# Mollick — from working with AI to managing AI

**What happened.** "Real AI Agents and Real Work": long-duration agentic work — hours of
work handed over, reasonable results back in minutes — marks the shift from working
*with* AI to *managing* AI. Mollick's claim: 99.9% of people's AI future is agentic.
His book *Co-Existence* lands 20 October.

**Why it matters.** The management frame is the one that transfers cleanly to a
regulated-industry audience, because they already know how to manage: delegation,
accountability, review, sign-off. If agentic work is a management problem rather than a
tooling problem, the existing governance muscle applies — which is the same move the AML
desk argument makes. The October book is a fixed date to write against.
