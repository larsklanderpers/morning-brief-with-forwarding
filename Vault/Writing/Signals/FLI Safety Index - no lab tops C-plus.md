# FLI Safety Index — no lab tops C+, and commitments are being rolled back

**What happened.** Anthropic C+ (highest), OpenAI and Google DeepMind C, Meta D+, with
xAI/DeepSeek/Mistral effectively failing. The finding that got less coverage: several majors
have **quietly walked back earlier safety commitments**.

**Why it matters.** For a regulated buyer this is the first citable, comparative governance
record on the labs — it turns "which vendor is safer" from a vibe into a line item in a
diligence pack. The rollback finding is the sharper half: a commitment that can be withdrawn
without consequence was never a control, and procurement teams treating published safety
policies as assurances are relying on something with no enforcement behind it. That is exactly
the argument for the FINRA-style body — and exactly why the FINRA critique matters.
