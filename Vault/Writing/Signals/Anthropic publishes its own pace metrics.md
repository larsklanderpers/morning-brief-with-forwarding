# Anthropic publishes its own pace metrics

**What happened.** Days after Dario Amodei argued the frontier should be paced (12 Sep), his own company
published the instrument by which it proposes to be watched, and built it itself. Three measurements:
an automation index (AL0 *no AI involvement* to AL5 *is autonomous*) on which **Claude "leads" 26% of
Anthropic's AI R&D work as of August 2026, up from under 1% in February**; an oversight panel for the
roughly **30,000 agents** running simultaneously on its platform (100% monitor coverage, a **0.002%**
block rate, about **50 high-priority flags a week** reaching a human); and a **6%** share of AI R&D
compute going to safety research, **12%** of the AI-driven share. Every figure is self-measured,
self-judged and unaudited. Third-party evaluators are future tense: *"we plan to embed"*.

**Why it matters.** [[Ad Hoc Regulatory Regime]] has so far collected improvised instruments written by
regulators who half-arrived and by consultants with nothing behind them. **This is the other kind: the
firm writing the measuring instrument for its own conduct, well and in public, because nobody else is
going to.** The page is unusually self-critical - the judge model can share the errors of the model it
judges, compute is an imperfect proxy, *"many of the underlying labels we relied on are best-effort,
not verified"*, one week is not a trend - and its most quotable sentence is an argument against the
company's own interest: *"safety research is hard to distinguish from capabilities research, and each
developer will be tempted to draw the line generously,"* so *"the burden of proof should sit with the
developer."* That is the strongest version of the other side, offered by the side itself.

**It does not close the gap the 20 Sep capstone names.** That piece went looking for a frontier lab
that offers an affected party a way *into* its own disclosure process and found none. This publishes
the record; it still does not open a route to contest it, and the party setting the scale is the party
being scored on it. **Corroborates, does not contradict** - but it is the best available counter-exhibit
and should be argued against rather than around.

**★ The pairing that is worth more than either number alone.** The same institute, the same lead
author, published *"When AI builds itself"* on 4 Jun 2026 and the **80% of merged code** figure that
the 19 Aug piece quoted. **Set 80% of code written beside 26% of work led and under 1% autonomous, and
the two resolve rather than conflict:** authorship is not direction. Anyone reaching for the 80% number
from here should carry the 26% in the same breath, because the vendor's own later instrument is what
puts the ceiling on it. Anthropic sells Claude; this is still the vendor measuring itself.

## Open

- **Is there a piece in the burden-of-proof sentence?** A lab arguing that the burden should sit with
  the developer, about the one number it most controls, is a different move from a lab publishing a
  framework it wrote for itself. Not yet placed.
- **The 0.002% block rate cuts both ways** and the note does not decide which: near-nothing is blocked
  because near-nothing needs to be, or because the monitors catch what they were built to look for.
  Anthropic says the honest half itself - at this scale it cannot be certain it has a complete picture.
