# Netflix CPTO — systems thinking as the AI-era product skill

**What happened.** Elizabeth Stone on managing AI-era product teams: the rise of systems
thinking, building excellence as an operating system, and how product and engineering
roles are changing under AI pressure.

**Why it matters.** Netflix is a useful bellwether — enterprise scale with genuinely high
data-sensitivity requirements, so it is not a startup anecdote. The systems-thinking frame
is a direct response to AI destroying the value of narrow task execution and rewarding
people who understand how components interact. That is the same claim the archetype
literature makes, arriving from an operator rather than a commentator.
