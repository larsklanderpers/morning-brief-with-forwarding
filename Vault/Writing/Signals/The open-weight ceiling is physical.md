# The open-weight ceiling is physical — HBM, energy, datacenter

**What happened.** AI Supremacy's "The Open Source AI China Problem Just Got Worse" argues
model supremacy now plays out in a token-efficient macro environment bounded less by algorithms
than by hardware: high-bandwidth memory, energy, and datacenter capacity are the binding limits.
The open-weight capability race has run into a physical wall.

**Why it matters.** This reframes sovereignty from "can you download it" to "can you run it."
Frontier weights being free does not democratise frontier inference if the HBM, power and
datacenter footprint to serve them stays concentrated in a handful of hands. That changes *who*
can actually operate a frontier model in-house — and it is the constructive counterpart to the
doom framing: capability growth hitting infrastructure walls reshapes the market for who runs
what, which is a Shape-of-Work-to-Come question, not just a security one.
