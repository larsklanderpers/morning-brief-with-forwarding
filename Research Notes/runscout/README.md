## First pass: the drafts against Lars's own feedback

Done from the page text and the intake summary of his evaluation. The docx's two embedded images and
the drafts' screenshots were not read, and desktop layout cannot be judged from text. This is what
jumps out, not the full review.

**Feedback the drafts have taken**

- The descriptor now leads with the spine: "the one durable record of what the team intended,
  changed, and decided." Close to his recommended entry line. "Attributable" has dropped out, and it
  was half his definition of the spine.
- The 4x footnote keeps the disclosure and adds "N=2".
- "Not vendor memory" in the not-this list is a sharp position against the managed-harness vendors.

**Feedback the drafts have not taken**

1. **Absolutes are still the headline.** His note said replace them. Draft 1: "There can be only
   one", "sees what is true now", "No drift", "Nothing important is lost", "Full parity", "Zero
   re-explaining". Draft 2: "the actual current state that governs every future action", "the
   re-briefing tax is eliminated". "True now" is exactly the unscoped "truth" his stress test asked
   to split into known, reported, inferred, contradicted, unknown.
2. **The anti-Scrum stamp is still there, now with "No bullshit" added.** He flagged it as narrowing
   the audience.
3. **The 4x is still a commit number sold as velocity.** And the same page says "Not a commit
   dashboard", so the headline stat undercuts the positioning two sections later.
4. **Pricing unit is still ambiguous.** "$14.99 per seat" plus "15 days, every seat, no card" against
   the price page's "per human developer, agent seats free". What happens on day 16 is not said, and
   the startup-offer limits are absent.
5. **Surveillance is asserted away, not designed away.** Draft 1 says "No surveillance". Draft 2's
   watchers "surface attention needs to human managers". His persona table gives the developer an
   adoption veto on exactly this fear; a claim in copy will not answer it, a visible scope rule would.

**New things the drafts introduce**

6. **The eight entry kinds have no decision, no correction, no handoff.** Draft 1 promises what the
   team "decided"; draft 2's taxonomy (architecture, dependency, deviation, intent, abandoned,
   convention, blocker, milestone) has no decision type, no supersession or correction type (his
   stress test's first gap), no way to hold disagreement, and no handoff or interface-contract type,
   which are the structured entries the live product documents. Worth asking Daniel which list is
   real.
7. **"Append-only log" is now in the copy**, which sharpens the retention contradiction he found
   (writing page: no edits or deletions; security page: configurable retention, verifiable
   deletion).
8. **"Connection to all agents via MCP"** is an integration-coverage claim, and his note found no
   public evidence of coverage. Same for naming Claude, Cursor and Copilot in the ticker.
9. **"Milestone: significant capability achieved"** is activity state. His point that merged is not
   deployed is not available to a customer is not in either draft.
10. **The design-partner quote is unnamed.** "Design partner, CTO" with no name or company reads as
    invented unless it is attributed or marked as paraphrase with permission.
11. **Unsourced figure:** "The model ignores 90% of it." Illustrative, but it sits next to a footnoted
    stat and borrows its credibility.
12. **Register.** The Highlander thread ("There can be only one", "When the quickening happens")
    is consistent and memorable, and it puts the single-truth absolute in the largest type on the
    page. His "Make standup human again" founder line appears in neither draft.
