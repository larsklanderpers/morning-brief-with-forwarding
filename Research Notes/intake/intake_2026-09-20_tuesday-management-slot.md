# Tuesday re-pointed to management and leading transitions, one-month trial from 29 Sep

## Lars's note

Verbatim, from `#heinlein-inbox`, 20 September 2026:

> Also I am thinking that what we need to do is make the Tuesday diversion (after this week) all about
> management with ai and leading transitions and the Saturday diversion continues to be about the personal
> ai. That feels more valuable. And we might even consider Tuesday -Thursday as management and Monday,
> Wednesday, Friday the drumbeat of cyber and AI and Sunday stays the capstone. Let's start with Tuesday
> next week (29th) and run for a month and then decide if we want to switch to a more active running
> management model.

## What is a decision and what is a musing

Read carefully, because the message carries three things at three different strengths and the charter
has to record them differently.

1. **DECIDED, and it has a start date.** Tuesday becomes management with AI and leading transitions,
   starting Tue 29 September 2026, running for a month, then reviewed. *"Let's start with Tuesday next
   week (29th) and run for a month."*
2. **CONFIRMED, no change.** Saturday continues to be the personal-AI slot. This is charter §3 as already
   written, so nothing moves; it is recorded here only so that a later seat does not read the Tuesday
   change as a licence to move Saturday too.
3. **OPEN, explicitly hypothetical.** Tue-Thu as a management block with Mon/Wed/Fri as the cyber-and-AI
   drumbeat and Sunday unchanged. His words are *"we might even consider"*, and the trial exists so that
   this gets decided on a month of evidence rather than now. This is the same question the charter already
   deferred as the Thursday-as-third-variation call, arriving from the other side.

## The date, and why there is no conflict

Today is Sunday 20 September. Read literally, "next week" is 21 to 27 September and its Tuesday is the
22nd, not the 29th. The reading that makes the message self-consistent is that "this week" is the week
being planned right now (21 to 27 Sep, plan written today) and "next week" is 28 September to 4 October,
whose Tuesday is the 29th. Both halves of the message agree under that reading.

**It also lands clean against what is already on disk**, which is the reason to take the 29th at face
value rather than query it. `week-plan_2026-09-21.md` was written today and Tue 22 is already settled by
his own ruling of the same day: broader politics, four strands, gate FAIL overruled to PASS, and the
research pass already done and footed in `research_tue22-local-politics_2026-09-20.md`. Starting the new
Tuesday on the 22nd would throw away a completed research pass and reverse a ruling he gave hours earlier.
Starting it on the 29th does not disturb anything. **The 29th is taken as his intent.**

## What it resolves that was already open

`week-plan_2026-09-21.md` line 114 to 116 carries a standing warning: Tue 22 is the second political
Tuesday in three, and *"if a third lands on 29 Sep, the gate fails on count."* This ruling pre-empts that
fail. Tue 29 is now a management piece by instruction, so the count never reaches three.

## The trial window and where it is decided

Four Tuesdays: **29 Sep, 6 Oct, 13 Oct, 20 Oct.** "A month" from the 29th closes after the 20 October
piece. The decision therefore belongs to **the session that writes the week plan for the week of Monday
26 October 2026** - which is exactly where charter §5 already puts the Thursday review and the
end-of-October corpus read. **One review, three questions, not two separate reviews.**

## Reading of "a more active running management model" - MINE, NOT HIS

He did not define the phrase and I did not ask. My reading is that it means the fuller Tue-Thu management
block from the sentence before it, that is, management as a running two- or three-day beat rather than one
day a week. **Recorded as an inference and flagged as one.** If it means something else, the October
review is where he says so, and until then the charter records both the phrase and this caveat.

## Thread it points at

`Vault\Writing\Threads\Adoption and Change.md` is the existing hub for this beat: how organisations
actually absorb AI, and why the change-management canon they reach for was built for a different kind of
change. Its sister hub is `Shape of Work-to-Come.md`, which is Monday's. **That adjacency is the risk the
gate now has to watch** and it is written into the charter as a fail signal: the new Tuesday is the
manager's job of leading people through the transition, Monday is what the work itself becomes.
