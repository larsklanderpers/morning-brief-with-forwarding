## What it is good for, and the one thing to fix

- **It is the empty state, so the draft's own note holds: nothing needs anonymizing.** No chat names,
  no people, no tenant, no employer, no message content. Verified against the DO-NOT-PRINT list in
  the draft header: none of it is in frame.
- **It corroborates two sentences already in the draft.** "Live Teams data" plus "Sign out" is the
  running-against-my-real-account claim, and "New chats will appear after the next refresh" is the
  refresh-every-couple-of-hours rule showing up in the product's own words.
- ⚠️ **It does not show what the caption asks for.** The draft's [GRAPHIC] note says *"the Caught up!
  screen with fireworks mid-burst"*, and his own quoted prompt in the piece says *"blow some fireworks
  for me"*. What is on screen is a **party popper**, and it is the settled state rather than a burst.
  A still cannot show a two-second animation mid-flight anyway. Two clean ways out, both his call:
  caption it honestly (the finish line, not the animation), or send a second frame grabbed during the
  celebration if one exists. **Do not caption this image "fireworks".**
- **Optional, and it cuts both ways: the dock badges are in frame** - Slack 3, Instagram 5, phone 1.
  A piece about capping unread counts, shot over a row of uncapped unread counts. It can read as the
  honest joke or as the thing a reader notices instead of the point. Crop the dock or keep it, but
  decide on purpose.
