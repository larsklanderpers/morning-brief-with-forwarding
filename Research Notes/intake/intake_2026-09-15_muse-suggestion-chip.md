**What the screenshot shows versus what his note claims, for the Sat 19 chat:**
- The bottom card is a **scripted onboarding tip** ending in a **one-tap suggested reply**. That is the
  approachability mechanic at its plainest: the user never has to know what to type.
- His stronger point, suggestions made unprompted from context, is better shown by the **top card**
  ("the call ... can happen whenever you're ready"), which is the private one. If he wants that beat
  pictured, it needs a redacted version of the top card, not this crop.
- Meta's own primary footing for "suggestions unprompted" is already in the research note (newsroom,
  8 Sep: it "remembers what matters to a person, so it can make suggestions unprompted").
- "I'll always check with you before I buy anything" is the user-facing face of the Sentinel approval
  model in the same note. Product copy, not a verified behaviour.
