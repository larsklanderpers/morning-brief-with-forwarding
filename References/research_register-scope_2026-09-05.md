# Research Note — NSW AI register scope: the exclusion, and where it sends you

For: Mon 7 Sep 2026 slot (rebuilt from the 8 Aug CPS 230 draft) · Depth: deep, primaries only · Date: 2026-09-05
Supersedes nothing. Extends `research_nsw-ai-operational-policy_2026-08-06.md` with a live re-verification
and one finding that note did not have.

## Bottom line

The 6 Aug note's central fact survives re-verification and is now stronger. The forty-word supplier
exclusion is **verbatim identical across two published versions of the policy**, so it is not an
artefact of a first draft. Somebody re-opened the document, incremented the version, and left the
sentence exactly where it was.

**The new finding is what happens downstream of it.** The exclusion does not merely remove supplier AI
from the register. It hands supplier AI to a track whose own governing documents still name the policy
and the circular that this policy replaced. Three NSW web properties, all live today, all pointing at
superseded authority. The register is current. Everything the register's scope sentence excludes is
routed into paperwork that is not.

Nobody has written this. It requires reading four NSW properties against each other, and each one looks
fine on its own.

## What is solid — all re-verified live, 5 Sep 2026

### 1. The operative policy and its version history

- **Circular DCS-2026-02**, status **Active**, issue date **30 July 2026**, replaces **DCS-2024-04**.
  Amended to reflect the all-of-government NSW AI Operational Policy, which replaces the NSW AI Ethics
  Policy, and the deployment of the AI Assessment Framework Platform.
  [arp.nsw.gov.au](https://arp.nsw.gov.au/dcs-2026-02-use-of-artificial-intelligence-by-nsw-government-agencies)
- **Two published versions of the policy PDF exist and both are still served:**
  - July file: version line *"Final version 1: July 2026"*.
    `.../sites/default/files/2026-07/nsw-ai-operational-policy-2026-v1_2.pdf`
  - Current file, linked from the live policy page: version line *"Final version 1.1 - 30 July 2026"*,
    served as `.../sites/default/files/2026-09/nsw-ai-operational-policy-01092026.pdf`.
- **Reading, and it is the careful one:** v1.1 is dated to the circular's issue date (30 July), which is
  the date the policy became operative. The file was republished under a September filename. **Do not
  print "the policy was amended on 1 September."** What is verified is that the version line moved from
  "Final version 1" to "Final version 1.1", and that the file currently served carries a September
  filename. What changed between the two versions is **UNVERIFIED** — no diff was performed and no
  change log is published. If the piece needs that, it needs a page-by-page comparison first.

### 2. The forty words, unchanged across both versions

> *"The requirement to register AI use cases does not apply to third-party suppliers providing services
> to the NSW Government where they use AI as part of those services. These arrangements are managed
> through procurement, contracting, and supplier assurance processes."*

Confirmed verbatim in **both** the July v1 file and the current v1.1 file. **This is the load-bearing
verification and it holds.** The claim available to the piece is therefore not "there is an exclusion"
but **"the exclusion survived a revision"**, which is a materially better fact and is footed twice.

### 3. The rest of the policy, re-quoted from the current v1.1 file

- *"AI use cases must be registered before use."*
- Accountable Official: *"Designate an Accountable Official (Senior Executive)... and notify NSW GovAI
  within three months of this policy being issued."*
- Attestation: *"by 31 October each year, commencing with the first reporting date of 31 October 2027"*
- Review Committee: *"Agencies retain full decision-making authority and accountability when applying
  this advice."*
- *"Treat all inputs to and outputs of AI systems as potentially shared with the provider."*
- *"Ensure AI dependencies supporting critical services have tested business continuity plans that can
  be activated when required."*
- *"Records must be managed as State records in accordance with the State Records Act 1998."*

### 4. ★ THE NEW FINDING — where the exclusion sends you, and what is waiting there

The exclusion routes supplier AI to *"procurement, contracting, and supplier assurance processes."*
All three of the documents that govern that route currently cite superseded authority.

**(a) AI procurement essentials — `UPDATED: 6 MAY 2026`**, stated on the page, four months stale as of
today. Reached by browser after the direct URL 404'd and a second URL was robots-blocked; the live page
is at `/resources/artificial-intelligence-ai-procurement-essentials`, and the URL cited in the 6 Aug
note (`/buying-for-nsw-government/ai-procurement-essentials`) now **returns Page Not Found**.
- Names, under "Other NSW Government policies and frameworks": Procurement Policy Framework, ICT
  Purchasing Framework, **AI Ethics Policy**, AI Strategy, AI Assessment Framework. The AI Ethics Policy
  is the document the Operational Policy replaced in terms.
- Names the relevant procurement type verbatim: *"Platform, service or outcome-based procurement
  involving AI: Contracting a supplier to deliver a specific outcome or deliverable, where the supplier
  utilises generative AI technologies as part of the service provision."*
- Says guidance is still coming: *"Guidance will be developed for responsible AI officers, and buyers"*
  and *"The AI Procurement Essentials, and the upcoming AI Procurement Guidance..."* Still upcoming.

**(b) Procurement Policy Framework — update history last entry 13 December 2024.** Its AI entry names
**DCS-2024-04**, the archived circular. Twenty-one months without an update on the framework that
carries statutory force under the *Public Works and Procurement Act 1912*.
[update history](https://www.info.buy.nsw.gov.au/policy-library/policies/procurement-policy-framework/update-history)

**(c) The AIAF landing page — still cites the dead circular.** The page hosting the mandatory assessment
tool the whole regime runs on:
> *"All NSW Government use of AI... must apply the NSW AI Assessment Framework (AIAF) in line with the
> NSW mandate under Circular DCS-2024-04 to use both the AI Ethics Policy and the AIAF."*
No reference to DCS-2026-02 or to the Operational Policy. No last-updated date shown. **Re-verified
today, one month after the 6 Aug check found the same thing.** It has now been stale for over a month
in observation and roughly fourteen months against the circular it cites.

## What is NOT verified, and must not print as fact

- **What changed between policy v1 and v1.1.** No diff, no published change log. UNVERIFIED.
- **Why the file carries a September filename.** Could be a CMS republish, a broken-link fix, an
  accessibility remediation, or a substantive reissue. UNVERIFIED. Do not speculate in print.
- **That anyone at NSW intended the downstream staleness.** The finding is the state of the record, not
  a motive. The 6 Aug note's discipline applies: this is a **visibility** argument, not a coverage
  argument. Supplier AI is covered at purchase. It is invisible in the register, and the documents
  governing the purchase point at superseded authority. Write the narrow claim.
- **Auditor-General.** AI governance audit scheduled 2026-27, has not reported. Re-check before ship;
  the 6 Aug status was the same and has not been re-confirmed today. TREAT AS UNRE-CHECKED.
- **Whether any of this has been noticed publicly.** Searched, nothing found. That is an unverified
  absence, not a fact, and it does not print as "nobody has noticed."
