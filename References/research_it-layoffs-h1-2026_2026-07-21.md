# Research Note — IT/Tech Layoffs, H1 2026 (Jan–Jun)
For: Sat 1 Aug entry-rung piece + Shape-of-Work thread · Depth: deep · Date: 2026-07-21

## Bottom line (3–4 sentences)
There is no single "H1 2026 tech layoffs" number — there are two defensible ones, and any use should name the tracker. **~120,000 laid off at tech companies globally (Layoffs.fyi, via TechCrunch 6 Jul)** — which nearly matches Layoffs.fyi's *entire* 2025 total (123,941) in six months. **139,156 announced U.S. tech-sector cuts (Challenger, Gray & Christmas H1 report, 2 Jul)** — up 83% year over year. And the counterweight that keeps the piece honest: tech *occupations* across the whole economy GREW in the same window (CompTIA/BLS: +47,000 in June alone, 2.9% tech unemployment) — gross tech-company cuts and net tech-employment growth are both true at once.

**Honest phrasing for a draft:** "between roughly 120,000 (tech-company layoffs globally, per Layoffs.fyi) and 139,000 (announced U.S. tech-sector cuts, per Challenger) in the first half of 2026 -- on pace for the worst year since 2023, with AI the most-cited stated reason for four straight months."

## The brief's standing figure — corrected
The briefs' recurring line "115,000 through May 2026, approaching all of 2025's 124,000" is **directionally right, now stale, and never directly citable**:
- 124,000 = Layoffs.fyi's 2025 total, precisely **123,941 at 269 companies** ✅
- "115K through May" = plausible interpolation (tracker stood at 102,695 on May 11, ~113,000 on May 18) but **no source prints exactly 115,000** — don't cite it as a figure.
- **Update for all future use: "roughly 120,000 in H1 -- nearly matching all of 2025 in six months"** (TechCrunch citing Layoffs.fyi, 6 Jul).
- "Meta, Amazon, Snap citing AI": Meta ✅ and Snap ✅ explicit; **Amazon framed its cuts as bureaucracy/culture with AI as enabler** — say "AI-adjacent," not "citing AI."

## What's solid
- **Challenger H1 2026: 139,156 announced U.S. tech-sector cuts, +83% vs H1 2025 (76,214); all-industry total 443,604.** Primary fetched: [Challenger June report, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.challengergray.com/blog/challenger-report-june-layoffs-cool-to-45849-down-53-from-may-ai-leads-reasons-for-fourth-consecutive-month/); corroborated [HR Dive](https://www.hrdive.com/news/tech-layoffs-surge-83percent-h1-2026-challenger-ai-disruption/824320/).
- **Layoffs.fyi ~120,000 YTD (as of ~6 Jul); 2025 total 123,941/269 companies.** [TechCrunch running list](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/06/the-running-list-major-tech-layoffs-in-2026-where-employers-cited-ai/), [Computerworld timeline](https://www.computerworld.com/article/3816579/tech-layoffs-this-year-a-timeline.html).
- **AI attribution (Challenger, the only tracker coding stated reasons): AI cited in 101,743 H1 cuts (~23% of all announcements), #1 stated reason four consecutive months through June** (June alone: 14,029 = 31%). Trajectory: ~7% of cuts in January → ~40% in May.
- **Amazon: ~16,000 corporate cuts Jan 2026, ~30,000 cumulative since Oct 2025** ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazon-confirms-16000-more-job-cuts-bringing-total-layoffs-to-30000-since-october/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/28/amazon-layoffs-anti-bureaucracy-ai.html)). Meta 8,000 (~10%, May 20). Snap 1,000 (16%, Apr 16).
- **CompTIA/BLS: tech OCCUPATIONS +47,000 in June, tech-occupation unemployment 2.9% (vs 4.2% national); May +69,000.** [CompTIA, 2 Jul](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/tech-hiring-momentum-continues-as-tech-occupations-and-new-job-postings-increase-comptia-analysis-reveals-302816985.html), [Dice](https://www.dice.com/career-advice/comptia-tech-jobs-report---june-2026).

## What's contested / watch out
- **TrueUp says 170,390** YTD in 475 rounds — broader inclusion criteria; consistently runs 40–100% above Layoffs.fyi. Name it only as the high-end tracker.
- **The Yahoo/Engadget ">165,000" tracker inflates** by counting multi-year programs in full (e.g. Oracle's 21,000-over-12-months, announced Apr, confirmed Jun — only a fraction lands in H1 headcount). Don't use.
- **Challenger counts announcements, not separations** — some never fully execute; some hit non-US staff of US firms. Layoffs.fyi counts tech *companies* (including recruiters, sales, HR), not tech *roles*.
- PayPal 4,500 vs 4,800 (both circulate); Block's "50% of workforce" claim is almost certainly wrong (use ~4,000).

## The economics
The definitional trap IS the economics story. Three numbers point in opposite directions: gross tech-company cuts at record pace (~120K global / 139K US announced), while net tech-occupation employment RISES (+47K in June, 2.9% unemployment) — because laid-off engineers get rehired (increasingly outside the tech sector) and the cuts fall heavily on non-technical roles at tech firms. And the AI-attribution number (101,743) measures the press release, not the P&L: AI is now the socially acceptable cover for demand- and cost-driven cuts, exactly as Altman's "AI-washing" line warned. Pairs with the entry-rung data: the aggregate holds; the on-ramp narrows; the cuts concentrate where the Grower pipeline lives.
