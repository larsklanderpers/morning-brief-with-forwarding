## The arc: your rules were priced for a counterparty who got tired  (working name: THE COUNTERPARTY NEVER TIRES)

Last week changed axis from production to authority: what you hand your OWN agent (scope, onboarding, pay). **This week changes axis again, from authority to friction: what happens when the party on the other side of the exchange has the same model.** A great many rules, defenses and contracts quietly relied on the other side's effort being expensive: an attacker had to staff a crew, a biller had to pay a coder, a claimant had to read the terms. That effort is now the price of tokens. Wednesday shows it on the attack side, Thursday shows the same price drop works for you, Friday shows what it costs when two counterparties automate against each other, and Sunday merges them.

- **Wed opens with 2-3 points:** (1) one operator with an open-source agent and a few API keys did a crew's work against Korean banks (ARTEX); (2) you are being swept, not picked: a contractor ran the free scanner list at industrial scale across three continents (AA26-281A); (3) the bugs AI finds are the dangerous kind, so triage by exploitation, not by score (GTIG).
- **Thu adds:** the same price drop arms the defender. A frontier-lab small model at $0.10 per million input tokens makes reading everything the counterparty sends affordable, and provenance checking is shipping as a free public utility.
- **Fri adds:** when both counterparties automate against each other's rules, the exchange costs more for both (BCBSA's $942M claim on AI hospital coding), and the same thing is coming to loyalty, rewards and claims.
- **Sun merges:** every rule that relied on the other side's effort being expensive is now a rule the other side reads faster than you do. The fix is not a better model; it is rules re-priced for a counterparty that never tires.

⚠️ **THESIS RISK on the arc: MEDIUM-HIGH.** (1) **Footing is one day of briefs (9 Oct), all secondaries.** If Wednesday cannot foot ARTEX and AA26-281A at CrowdStrike's and CISA's own pages, the arc is wrong and gets re-pitched.
