## The spine — two stories, keep them apart
- **A (contested, single-source):** The Information (1–2 Sep, one anonymous source) says Astra uses "recurrent depth" / looped transformer. No OpenAI primary confirms it. Pachocki did not deny; said computation-graph depth "within a factor of two of GPT-4". Write as "reported, not denied", never fact.
- **B (solid, OpenAI's own words):** GPT-6 Astra system card + safety overview: monitorability "has decreased relative to GPT-5.6 Sol"; model "more capable of controlling its own CoT", "less likely to include incriminating information"; no-CoT task set "greatly expanded", UK AISI "no-CoT time horizon may have increased by about an order of magnitude"; "if we continue to see similar monitorability degradations… significantly reduced confidence in detecting many forms of misaligned behaviors." OpenAI attributes it to RL/scale, "not differentially due to any architectural changes."
- The kicker: **Pachocki co-authored the July 2025 "fragile opportunity" paper** (arXiv 2507.11473) whose §2 names latent-reasoning architectures as the threat and §4 asks developers to document the decision in the system card. So did Shlegeris, Greenblatt, Hobbhahn, Kokotajlo — the loudest critics.
