**Slack drops go to `#heinlein-out`. Always, unless he names somewhere else in the same message.**

Established 8 September 2026, his word, after a Thursday draft was posted to his Slack self-DM because
he had asked for it to be "slacked" without naming a channel.

**Why:** he has seven channels and the destination is not guessable from their names. Asking which one
spends a turn on something he has already decided; guessing lands a draft in the wrong place. He settled
it as a standing rule so neither happens again. His exact framing: *"its fine where it is, but by
standard, unless i tell you differently, drop it always into heinlein-out."*

**How to apply:**

- "slack it", "send it to slack", "post it" with no channel named -> `#heinlein-out`. Do not ask.
- A named channel in his message wins for that message only; the standing rule is not overwritten by it.
- Email delivery goes to lars.klander@gmail.com with the full draft text in the body, not as an
  attachment -- he reads these on a phone. Put the flags he needs before reading at the top of the body,
  above the piece.
- **Sending anything is still RED.** This rule settles the destination, never the decision. He has to ask
  for the delivery before either channel is used.
- The rule also lives in `CLAUDE.md` under AMBER so a cold start sees it without reading memory.
