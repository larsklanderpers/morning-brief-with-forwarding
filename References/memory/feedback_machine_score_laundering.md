Two new failure shapes, both found in the 9 Sep 2026 story-candidate triage. **Neither is caught by
engines 1-7 in [[brief-claim-provenance]], nor by [[estimand-substitution]].** Check them alongside.

## Engine 10 — MACHINE-SCORE LAUNDERING

**A number generated by an automated enrichment pipeline is read as an authoritative assessment, and
then the *vector* is inferred backwards from the number.**

**The case.** CVE-2026-19593, "OpenAI Codex Desktop, CVSS 9.8 critical." All true-looking, and:

- The **CNA container (OpenAI, shortName `OAI`) carries no CVSS at all.** OpenAI assigned no severity.
- The 9.8 sits in the **ADP container**, provider **`CISA-ADP`**, typed **`Secondary`** in NVD's JSON.
- There is **no metric entry sourced to `nvd@nist.gov`.** NVD reads `vulnStatus: Undergoing Analysis`.
- CISA-ADP scored it **`AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N`** — *no user interaction* — on a bug whose vendor text says
  it requires *"the user to open the attacker-prepared repository with its local Git configuration."*
- **The same ADP record sets SSVC `Exploitation: none`.** The machine contradicts itself in one record.
- The brief then wrote *"the score indicates unauthenticated RCE or equivalent"* — **inferring the attack
  vector from the number**, which is engine 7 running backwards.

**Why it needs its own engine.** Every provenance check passes. The CVE is real, the ID is right, the
product is right, the score is genuinely in the record, and the source is a government agency. Nothing
was dropped, substituted, or laundered by proximity. **The failure is that an automated enrichment score
was read as an assessment by a person.**

### ★★ And it is not decorative — since 10 June 2026 it is binding (confirmed at the regulator)

**CISA's Binding Operational Directive 26-04, *Prioritizing Security Updates Based on Risk*, issued
10 June 2026**, sets federal remediation timelines from four decision variables — Asset Exposure, KEV
Status, Exploit Automation, Technical Impact — and then says, verbatim:

> *"CISA publishes answers to KEV Status, Exploit Automation, and Technical Impact for every CVE ID
> through services such as the **Vulnrichment Program**."*

Table 1's own footnote repeats it: those elements are *"provided to the CVE database through CISA's
Vulnrichment Program for every vulnerability with a CVE ID."*

**So the same automated pipeline that stamped 9.8 / `UI:N` on the Codex bug is the input to a binding
federal patch clock.** The machine score is load-bearing, and the person who would once have sanity-
checked it has been removed from the loop at both ends of the year:

- **15 April 2026** — NIST stops routinely issuing severity scores outside KEV / federal-software /
  EO-14028 scope. The human-analysed layer goes dark for the long tail.
- **10 June 2026** — BOD 26-04 makes the machine-generated layer the trigger for a compulsory clock.

⚠️ **Two collateral facts to carry:** **BOD 26-04 revokes BOD 19-02 and BOD 22-01**, so every
*"KEV means 15 days under BOD 22-01"* sentence in circulation has been wrong since June — the catalog
survives, the directive behind it does not. And **the directive does not use CVSS in its timelines at
all**; it runs on SSVC, mentioning CVSS only to say technical impact is *"similar to"* the CVSS base
score's concept of severity. **The number everyone still quotes was demoted at both ends of the system
in the same year.**

★ **A bonus, and it inverts the usual pattern.** CISA hedges where the vendors assert: its own
background reads *"their use of AI **may further narrow** the time defenders have to react."* Meanwhile
ZDI and Tenable attribute a record ~970-CVE September to AI-assisted discovery and **Microsoft says
nothing at all**. **The regulator writing the binding rule is the most cautious voice in the story** —
the structural finding at the end of [[brief-claim-provenance]] running in reverse.

**How to apply — three questions on every CVSS number:**
1. **Which container is the score in** — CNA, ADP, or NVD? Read the JSON, not the web page (nvd.nist.gov
   and cve.org are JS-rendered and return nothing; **the APIs are the primaries**:
   `cveawg.mitre.org/api/cve/<ID>` and `services.nvd.nist.gov/rest/json/cves/2.0?cveId=<ID>`).
2. **Does the vector agree with the vendor's own description of the bug?** `UI:N` against "the user must
   open" is the tell. So is `PR:N` against "authenticated".
3. **Is it on KEV?** If not, `cisaExploitAdd` is absent and the ADP's own SSVC usually says
   `Exploitation: none` — **two structured negatives that a "critical" headline hides.**

**Correct wording when the score is ADP-only:** *"scored 9.8 by CISA's automated ADP enrichment; the
assigning CNA published no severity score of its own, and NVD has not analysed it."*

★ **And the correction is sharper than the hedge, as always.** Not "critical Codex RCE" but *an automated
scoring pipeline stamping 9.8 with no-user-interaction on a bug the vendor says needs a click — and
since June that pipeline's answers start a compulsory federal clock.*

**Related, same day, same shape:** KEV `requiredAction` and `dueDate` are now **variable and
risk-tiered** — internet-facing appliances got **three days** (added 2 Sep, due 5 Sep) against **fourteen**
for the 8 Sep Microsoft zero-days. **Read the KEV record's own fields rather than assuming the old
three-week default.** And `knownRansomwareCampaignUse: Unknown` means **do not write "ransomware."**

## Engine 11 — PREDICATE MANUFACTURE (the proper noun survives verification)

**The named artefact is real and correctly named. The thing it is said to DO was invented in the
summarising step.**

**The case.** *"the post highlights Anthropic's Model Hardware Standard as an emerging protocol-layer
safeguard [for] multi-agent coordination."* The Model Hardware Standard **exists** — Anthropic published
*"Previewing the Model Hardware Standard"* on 27 Aug 2026, *"a shared specification for AI agents to
safely operate physical devices."* It is for **drug discovery, protein design, quantum computing and
microscopy**. It contains **no mention of multi-agent coordination, agent-to-agent communication, side
channels, or containment.**

**Why it defeats the existing checks.** A proper-noun search — the standard defence against engine 5 and
engine 6 — **passes**. The name is in the vendor's own primary, on the vendor's own domain, dated
correctly. Engine 7 catches an added *adjective* ("first", "largest"); this adds an added **function**.
There is no upstream sentence to diff against because the relationship was manufactured.

**How to apply.** **Verify the predicate, not just the proper noun.** Open the artefact's own page and
search it for the *capability being claimed* — the verb, not the name. If the document does not describe
the thing it is credited with doing, **cut the whole clause, and everything built on top of it.** In this
case a governance argument was resting on the relationship and had no factual base at all.

★ **The generalised form:** engine 6 says *verify the claim inside the source*. This says **verify the
capability inside the artefact.** A real thing can be cited for a job it does not do.

## Two more from the same pass, worth their own lines

**A social post by a non-participant restating events is a reader, not a source.** The 9 Sep item cited
one by name; **the post could not be verified to exist at all**, and the person has no affiliation with
any party to the events. **Everything true in the item was available from the actual primaries** (METR,
Redwood, OpenAI). **When an item's only citation is an individual commenting on somebody else's
incident, go to the incident.**

★ **The weld can run the other way too — a secondary can turn a historical reference into a live quote.**
Caught live on 9 Sep: the FT's *"Jan Leike, who co-led the now-defunct 'superalignment' team, **resigned
in 2024** citing his view that safety was taking a 'back seat to shiny products'"* became, in The Verge's
16 Aug rendering, *"leading some critics like Jan Leike... **to tell FT** that the company was ignoring
safety."* **A two-year-old resignation statement, re-dated to now, in the same paragraph as a correct
link.** Engine 1 plus the date trap, in a top-tier outlet, still uncorrected three weeks on.
