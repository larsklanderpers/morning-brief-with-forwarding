3–5 concurrent narrative threads rotating across the week. **Sunday capstone** (heavy) synthesises the
week. **Saturday "Diversions"** (light / operational / how-to) exist to counter doom-fatigue. Thread
**"Shape of Work-to-Come"** is the constructive tributary, fed by the 8am ai-work-community-brief.

**A Diversion is judged by its state of being, not its length** (Lars, 12 Aug 2026): light means the
optimistic take is load-bearing and **the reader leaves with something they can do.** A 3,300-word one
that lands on agency is doing its job; a short one that lands on dread is not.

## ★ THE ENCYCLOPAEDIA-OF-SKILLS RULING (Lars, 23 Aug 2026)

His words: *"i think talking about skills regularly is acceptable, as long as we bookend it properly.
if we try and say every week is the same skill, that's a problem. if each skill is part of an
encyclopaedia of skills, its a whole different animal."*

**This supersedes the reasoning that treated two how-to pieces in adjacent weeks as an inherent
collision.** The unit of repetition is **the skill**, not **the category of piece.**

- **Running a skill piece regularly is a FEATURE.** An encyclopaedia is cumulative. The recurrence of
  the *form* is what makes it a series — readers learn the shape and come back for it.
- **The failure is the same skill twice**, dressed differently. That is the thing to check for.
- **Bookending is the mechanism that makes the difference legible.** Without it, the third entry reads
  as *"he is saying that again."* With it, it reads as *"this is the third one."* **So a skill piece
  should say, in one clause, where it sits in the set.**

### ⚠️ The sharpening this needs, or it will be over-applied

**This ruling licenses the SUBJECT recurring. It does not license the WORDING recurring — and it makes
the wording check MORE important, not less.**

The Sat 29 draft failed the panel categorically on *"That is the whole skill,"* against Sat 22's
*"The whole skill is telling the two apart."* That was never a category collision — both pieces teach
genuinely different skills. It was a **phrase** collision, in the same payoff position, on consecutive
Saturdays. If the form is going to recur deliberately, then **the specific language and the specific
skill are the only things distinguishing one entry from the next.** Repeated phrasing collapses the
entries back into one piece, which is exactly what the encyclopaedia frame is trying to avoid.

**Two checks, and they are independent:**
1. **Is this a different skill from the last one?** (the encyclopaedia test — his ruling)
2. **Does it share imagery, closing moves or distinctive phrasing with anything in the window?**
   (the [[Metaphor Bleed Across Consecutive Pieces]] test — unchanged, and still categorical)

### How to apply in the week plans

- **Stop writing constraints of the form "this must not be a second how-to piece."** Write "this must
  not be the same skill as X" and name the skill.
- **When two skill pieces are adjacent, bookend rather than separate them.** A one-clause signpost
  that names the previous entry does the work a slot change was being used to do.
- Worth raising with him when the set is large enough: if these really are encyclopaedia entries, they
  would benefit from being findable as a set — a recurring name, or an index post. Not built; his call.

### Already amended by this ruling

`References\week-plan_2026-08-31.md`, Sat 5 Sep block. Its constraint read *"Sat 29 was how to read a
claim. This Saturday must not be a second how-to-read-a-claim piece."* The **conclusion was right and
the reasoning was wrong** — Sat 5 (how the work is designed) is a different skill from Sat 29 (how a
claim is read), so it was never in danger. The fix is to **bookend Sat 5 as the next entry**, not to
hold it at arm's length.
