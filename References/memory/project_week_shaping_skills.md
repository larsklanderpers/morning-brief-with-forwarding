## ★ TIER 0 BOOKENDING — ADOPTED 23 Aug 2026 (Lars: "fair. do it.")

Standing convention for every piece that hands the reader a skill (the Tuesday ground-game and
Saturday long-form Diversions).

**The rule, and it is the whole of what was adopted:** one clause, early in the piece, naming the
previous entry and the skill it handed over. Nothing else.

**What is explicitly NOT adopted:** a name for the series, a marker line, numbering, or an index post.
Those are Tiers 1–3 in `References\SKETCH_encyclopaedia-of-skills.md` and they are **deferred to an
end-of-September review**, not rejected. Do not creep toward them.

**Why bookending alone:** without it, entry three reads as *"he is on about this again."* With it, it
reads as *"this is the third one."* Same content, different animal — and the difference costs one
sentence and creates no obligation to feed a series.

**It starts with Sat 5 Sep and does NOT run backwards.** Mon 24, Tue 25 and Sat 29 were signed off
and scheduled before the convention existed; retro-fitting them would be restructuring live work for
no reader benefit. The set accumulates forward.

⚠️ **The risk that made him hesitate, and it is his own history — keep it in view.** A named series
becomes a slot, and a slot gets filled whether or not the story is there. That is exactly how the
reader's-calculus close ran four straight weekdays in July: a stale charter listed it as a
requirement rather than a callback. His law is that **devices are seasoning, never a slot to fill.**
Tier 0 is safe precisely because it creates no slot. Anything above Tier 1 does.

**If the review ever goes past Tier 1:** write the retirement condition at the same time as the name
— *this runs when there is a real skill to hand over, and a month with none is a normal month.*
Cheap to write then, nearly impossible to add once readers expect a cadence.
