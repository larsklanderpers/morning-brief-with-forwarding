Obsidian vault lives at `D:\vc-personal-projects\Morning Brief with Forwarding\Vault\Writing\` (created 2026-07-20). Structure: `Threads/` (one hub per thread from the SUBSTACK_INDEX legend), `Signals/` (atomic notes, one per promoted story), `Templates/signal.md`, and `000 Vault Guide.md` holding the conventions.

**Why:** the daily briefs are a chronological archive that can't accumulate an argument across weeks. Thread hubs let evidence pile up per narrative line, so writing a piece means opening one hub instead of re-reading eleven brief files. It also gives [[project-writing-flow]]'s research-assistant local signal to search before fanning out to the web.

**How to apply:** feed policy is **flagged items only** — what Lars stars in the Brief Tracker, nothing else. Never bulk-import briefs; the hubs stop being readable within a fortnight. When a story recurs (Kimi K3 has appeared 4x), append a dated line to the existing signal note under `## Observations` — do not create a second note. Every signal links to at least one `Threads/` hub. Prune on the Sunday capstone pass: promote load-bearing signals into the hub's *State of play*, merge duplicates, mark spent signals `status: used`.

Note: Lars also runs a separate corporate Obsidian vault. This one is deliberately distinct — personal Substack research should not commingle with work-tenant data. Related: [[project-substack-pipeline]], [[project-content-architecture]], [[project-morning-brief]].
