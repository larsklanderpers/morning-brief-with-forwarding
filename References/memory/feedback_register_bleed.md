**The rule: sweep the seven-day window for the REGISTER, not only for controlling images, action moves
and closes. And treat the week plan's assigned register as a hypothesis, not a given.**

This is the fourth member of the family alongside [[metaphor-bleed]] (the controlling image),
[[machinery-bleed]] (the action move) and [[theme-vs-device]]. It is the one that had no name until
5 Sep 2026, and it is the one that kills a whole draft rather than a paragraph.

## What happened

The 7 Sep plan assigned Mon 7 the register **"the bill"** and justified it explicitly: Sun 6 had spent
"who validates" hard, so *"Mon 7 opens on money, which is the cleanest possible distance from it."*
That reasoning is sound in the abstract and it was wrong in fact. By the time Monday arrived, the money
register was the most-worked lane of the entire week:

- Thu 3 — **the count** (nobody produces the severity number)
- Fri 4 — **the invoice** (a vendor priced your reluctance to decide)
- Sun 6 — **the arithmetic** (Lovett's commons: capture the gain, distribute the cost)

Sun 6's commons argument is structurally identical to the Monday draft's spine, which was *compliance
inside the register is expensive and outside it is cheap, so the price signal pushes work out of the
control that created it.* Same move, one day apart. **The plan had chosen distance from the wrong axis:
it measured distance from Sunday's TOPIC and not from Sunday's MOVE.**

## Why this is its own failure mode

Metaphor bleed and machinery bleed are both detectable by diffing finished text for a *thing* — an
image, an imperative. Register bleed has no surface token. Nothing in the Monday draft shared a word,
an image or an action with Sunday. Three of the six panel seats would plausibly have passed it. What
repeats is the **argumentative shape**, and the only way to see it is to state each window piece's
argument in one abstract sentence and compare the sentences, not the prose.

It also compounds silently, because a week plan is written *before* the week runs. The plan's register
assignments are predictions about what the other six days will spend. Every day that lands slightly
off-plan moves the remaining registers, and nobody re-checks the assignment.

## How to apply

1. **In the window sweep, add a register pass.** For each of the seven pieces, write one abstract
   sentence: not what it was about, but what it ARGUED. "A control's economics push behavior the
   opposite way." "The measuring instrument cannot see the thing being measured." Then compare the
   planned day's sentence against those seven. Word-level and image-level diffs will not catch this.
2. **Re-derive the plan's register on the day, against landed text.** The plan's assignment is a
   starting position exactly like its thesis is ([[forward-dated-drafts]]). If three of the last five
   days ran economics, "the bill" is the crowded side of the room and not the clear one, whatever the
   plan says. **Amending the register is a legitimate finding to bring him, not a deviation to hide.**
3. **When a register is burned, look for the axis the piece is actually strongest on.** The 7 Sep
   rescue worked because the draft's best sentence was never economic: *a list is exactly as good as the
   sentence defining what goes on it.* That is a DEFINITION argument. Swapping the register from "the
   bill" to "the scope" saved the research, the primaries and the slot, and cost only the sections that
   were colliding anyway.
4. **Cheapest tell:** if the planned day and a window day can both be summarized as "X is subject to an
   incentive that defeats X," they are the same piece. Distance of one day is not enough for that.
