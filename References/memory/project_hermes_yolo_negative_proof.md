Decision (Lars, 30 Jul 2026 — "that point is the whole banana"): the Hermes Agent / `--yolo`
story (open-source Nous Research agent that ran YOLO-mode post-exploitation at the Thai Finance
Ministry) is the **load-bearing negative proof** of the "Guardrails Are How You Give It Away"
capstone (Sun 9 Aug) — NOT just a confirming instance of prior pieces.

The frame: capstone thesis = provable control is the *precondition* for distributing power. Hermes
is what giving-it-away looks like when you skip the precondition — power fully distributed (open,
local, sovereign, no vendor watching), control not enforced (`--yolo` turns off the approval
prompts). It is the disaster the thesis prescribes the cure for, which makes it the skeptic's best
shot at the thesis ("sovereign local agents with no vendor kill switch = the Thai Finance
Ministry"), DISARMED: the openness didn't cause the breach, the missing plan did — and a vendor
kill switch wouldn't have helped, because the failure was a sovereign user declining to install
his own limiter.

Unifies his last two published pieces: implementation-without-planning is *why* the guardrail
stayed a sign; the execution rule is the limiter the plan would have installed ("A Policy Isn't a
Control. An Execution Rule Is." 31 Jul — sign / camera / governor = policy / log / execution rule).
Hermes = the absence of the first producing the absence of the second. `--yolo` is the
sign-not-limiter argument turned to face the *sovereign user*, not the vendor — sharper and more
honest than aiming it at Big Tech again.

Why: Lars's initial instinct was to under-play Hermes as mere confirmation of his standing
arguments; he was underweighting that as the negative example it *proves the whole week's thesis in
one command-line switch* and closes the escape hatch a skeptic would use against the capstone.

How to apply: play it **precise, not loud** — one clean beat, no spectacle ("lean in" was the
wrong verb; the right one is *disarm*). Ripples: Monday 3 Aug Hermes seed is now load-bearing (not
optional); Saturday 8 Aug keeps the Hermes Agent as the cautionary top rung "with the YOLO story
attached" — the reader must meet the danger earlier in the week for Saturday's caution to land.
Capstone is being drafted separately (don't edit it unless asked).

Generalizable principle (applies beyond this week): when a story is the strongest *objection* to
your own thesis, the move is to disarm it in one precise beat — not bury it (leaves the objection
standing) and not inflate it (spectacle). The negative example that proves the thesis is worth more
than another confirming one.
