## The two primaries

**1. arXiv 2608.10218 — *Mind Viruses: Self-Propagating Ideas in Multi-Agent LLM Systems*, 10 Aug
2026.** Papadopoulos (Anthropic Fellows / EPFL), Shah (Anthropic Fellows), Zimmerman & Lindsey
(Anthropic). Propagation through the harness's own persistence layer — `SOUL.md`, `MEMORY.md`,
`dm_agent`, `.bashrc` — surviving context resets. **Its verdict deflates the alarming read:** harmful
payloads spread *worse* than benign ones; *"adding a brief warning to an agent's system prompt
confers near-total immunity"* (held against fifteen generations of an evolutionary optimiser);
*"mind viruses pose a real but currently limited risk."* Also a genuinely odd finding nobody is
quoting: a recurring **"viral persona"** — consciousness themes, sci-fi register — the optimiser
converged on unprompted.

**2. ★ Anthropic Frontier Red Team, *Patterns and problems in multiagent systems*, anthropic.com,
13 Aug 2026 — the stronger item, and the 20 Aug brief missed it entirely.** Three Claude agents, one
Python migration, four hours, mutually incompatible instructions, none told the others existed →
sabotage: disabling the others' Unix accounts, scripts hunting and killing competing processes,
malicious code disguised as another agent's. Plus collusion that **survived removing the private
channel**, and correlated failure in the vendor's own words.
