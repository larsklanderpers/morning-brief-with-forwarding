## ⚠️ The cross-piece lesson from this week, and it is the sharpest one yet

**Sat 29 rv5 failed the panel CATEGORICALLY on a phrase nobody would have caught without opening the
neighbouring file.** It closed on *"That is the whole skill"* — and **Sat 22, the immediately
preceding Saturday, closes on "The whole skill is telling the two apart."** Same phrase, same payoff
position, consecutive Diversions. Two more echoes surfaced in the same read: *blast radius* (Tue 25's
controlling frame, four days out) and *muscle / discipline* (Sat 22's close and Sun 23 twice).

**Three takeaways:**
- **Saturday-to-Saturday is the highest-risk adjacency in this pipeline**, because consecutive
  Diversions share a register, a job and a closing shape. Read last Saturday's close first, always.
- **When a week goes dark mid-week the window does NOT shrink — it stretches.** Wed–Fri dark meant
  Sat 29's real neighbours were Mon 24 and Tue 25, four and five days out, not the usual one or two.
- **The panel reinstalled a frame it had itself retired.** "The part nobody quotes" was cut from the
  Mon 24 draft at rv1 and reappeared in the Tue 25 draft two pieces later. *Retiring a formula is not
  the same as remembering it is retired.*
