**DOWN:** retail trade −154.6, wholesale trade −86.2, private education and health −96, professional

series has it shedding jobs through August. **Different quantities, different clocks, both true.**
Quoting either without the other is the cherry-pick the capstone exists to object to.

## ⚠️ ENGINE VARIANT — THE INSTRUMENT WELD

The 28 Aug brief ran the May/June **−103,000 monthly revisions** (July Employment Situation, released
**7 Aug**) under the label *"the BLS benchmark revision"* and called it *"Released this morning."*
Numbers correct; label and date wrong. **Monthly revisions and the annual benchmark are different
instruments** — the first is late survey responses and seasonal re-factoring, the second re-anchors the
whole level to QCEW once a year for a March reference month.

★ **The weld is what caused the miss.** Having "already covered the benchmark revision," the brief had
no reason to look for the real one landing that morning. **A category error does not just mislabel an
item — it can blind the pipeline to the real one.** Generalise: *when a brief claims to carry a named
periodic release, check that instrument's own release calendar.*
