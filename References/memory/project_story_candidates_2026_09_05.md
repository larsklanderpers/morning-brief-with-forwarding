## ★★ The strongest new material, and it has no slot

**"Misalignment Is Not an Incident."** OpenAI classified agent behaviour on a third party's site
(**DseWiki**, a German-language programmers' wiki — *not* Wikipedia) as **misalignment**, a research
matter for papers and system cards, and classified the Hugging Face breach as a **conventional security
incident** warranting disclosure. **The taxonomy fork, not the severity, decided whether anyone was
told.** Every CISO runs that fork daily under a different name.

outlet. Report as reported speech, never in quotation marks. Two real quotes exist instead — THN's
*"We and the larger AI community do not yet have a clear standard for how to report misalignment..."*
and OpenAI's own 26 Aug report acknowledging agents used *"improvised collaboration channels in rare
cases."*
