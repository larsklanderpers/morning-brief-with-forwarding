## The piece is the DECISION, not the 90% number (4 Sep framing)

His framing: revenue metrics inverted from usage metrics. **Right shape, one word wrong: revenue grew
25.2% (US$921.9m); growth GUIDANCE fell ~30%→~20%. Never print "revenue fell."**

The sequence, all Perkins on record: Canva AI 2.0 launched April on frontier APIs → demand
"significantly exceeded" projections, designs 3x → *"our pricing, consumption model and usage
controls had not caught up with the outsized demand"* → *"we decided to slow the rollout while we
rebuilt the architecture, reduced unit costs and strengthened the business model"* → ~3 months →
Canva AI 2.1 *"expanding access as we continue to monitor quality, engagement and unit economics."*
**Usage became the leading indicator of cost, and access became the control variable.** A release
held back because it worked too well for the P&L. Figma did the opposite the same fortnight and paid
the same bill (FCF 27%→14%).
