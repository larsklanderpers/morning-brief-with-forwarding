## The findings that carry it
1. **The 70% change-failure statistic is unsourced, not disputed.** Kotter's 1995 HBR paper contains
   no percentage. Chain: Hammer & Champy 1993 ("unscientific estimate," later disowned) → Beer &
   Nohria 2000 → textbook 2004 → Todnem By 2005 *inside a critical review* → Kotter's own 2008
   version (failure defined to include "over budget" and "late," no data) → McKinsey 2009 →
   peer-reviewed nursing journal 2019 sourced to a consultancy blog. Hughes (2011, *JoCM* 11(4))
   reviewed all five published sources and found nothing.
2. **Kotter superseded his own model in 2014** (*Accelerate*): concurrent + continuous, volunteer
   army, dual operating system. Almost every deck in circulation teaches the 1996 version.
3. **ADKAR has never been independently validated**; the one external quantitative test
   (Caekebeke et al. 2021, n=30) found no association between ADKAR scores and the behaviour.
   Keep the **barrier point**; drop the validation claim. ADKAR is 1 of 3 Prosci models and
   contains no leadership at all.
4. **AI adoption has no Desire problem** — 78% BYOAI; 90% of companies have shadow use vs 40% with
   licences. The sponsor is *behind* the workforce. The change dies at **Ability** (judgment, and
   self-report provably fails — METR 19% slower / believed 20% faster) and **Reinforcement**
   (workslop: usage up, quality down, dashboard green).
