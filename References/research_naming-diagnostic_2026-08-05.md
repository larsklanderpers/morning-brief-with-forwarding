## CONTESTED / HANDLE WITH CARE

**The two 32%s must never corroborate each other.** Robert Half (US hiring managers, *same or similar role*, no methodology) and Orgvue (300 US HR managers, *rehire staff* generally, Sept 2025) are different measurements. The coincidence that both land on 32% is doing real damage downstream — https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/07/01/companies-reverse-ai-driven-layoffs-as-human-workers-return-483875/ puts Orgvue's 55% and Robert Half's 32% in one paragraph, and that adjacency is where the conflation is manufactured. Do not average, cross-cite, or present as two studies agreeing.

**Orgvue 55% is REGRET, not rehiring, and it's 2025.** https://www.orgvue.com/news/55-of-businesses-admit-wrong-decisions-in-making-employees-redundant-when-bringing-ai-into-the-workforce/ — published 29 Apr 2025, fielded Feb–Mar 2025, n=1,163 C-suite/senior leaders across US/CA/UK/IE/AU/HK/MY/SG (Vitreous World).
Exact: *39% of business leaders made employees redundant as a result of deploying AI; of those, 55% admit they made wrong decisions about those redundancies.*
WARNING — **base trap:** 55% **of the 39%** is roughly **21% of all leaders surveyed**, not "55% of companies." TechRadar, Gadget Review and aibase all get this wrong. The release contains **no rehiring data at all.**

**Visier has no AI rehiring stat.** Their Layoff Boomerang work (2.4M employees, 142 companies; 5.3% overall rehire rate; high performers return at 120% higher rate; managers 68% higher) is **general** layoff boomerang data with **no AI variable**. Citing Visier as AI-rehiring evidence is a category error several blogs are committing. https://www.visier.com/lp/layoffs-boomerang-employees/

**Forbes "20 agentic categories" — the split is EVEN, not non-technical-majority.** https://www.forbes.com/sites/sandycarter/2026/06/02/the-20-new-agentic-ai-jobs-box-mckinsey-and-linkedin-all-see-coming/ (Sandy Carter, 2 Jun 2026): **10 technical, 10 non-technical**. The "non-technical outnumbers technical" framing comes from a separate McKinsey line in the same piece — *"the largest new category is not engineers, it is AI-augmented frontline workers"* — a claim about **volume**, not category count. `work_brief_2026-08-05.md` states it as a property of the 20-category list, which is wrong. Defensible only as the McKinsey volume claim.
Also in that piece: LinkedIn (Jan 2026) 1.3M+ new AI roles; 639,000 new AI postings of which AI Engineer is 75,000; WEF 170M created / 92M displaced by 2030.

**FDE posting growth — index artefact, and RESERVED FOR THURSDAY.** Indeed data via Business Insider (18 May 2026): April 2025 +543% vs Jan 2025 baseline; April 2026 **+5,230%** vs that baseline, *"roughly a 729% year over year"*; Indeed pay ~$170K–$200K+. https://www.businessinsider.com/forward-deployed-engineer-jobs-in-demand-2026-5

## ECONOMICS FRAMING (per the capability+cost rule)

The cost side of this piece is not the price of inference. It is **the cost of the walk-back**: severance paid, then a rehire at market rate for a role you just told the market you had automated, plus the recruiting cycle and the ramp. Ford's version is ~350 engineers over three years and, per Poon, the specific loss of experience *before it could train the systems meant to replace it* — the cut destroyed the training data for the automation that justified the cut. That is the concrete factor-of-N story here, and it is a cost story, not a capability one.

### BORROWED, WITH CREDIT: the wishing/washing pair
Averill's distinction, which this piece did not previously have:
- **AI wishing** -- the sincere belief that the technology is magic and you can wave it at a hard problem and skip the work. She calls it sincere, "and that is what makes it dangerous."
- **AI washing** -- its "insidious cousin," claiming to do more with AI than you actually are, under pressure to show results.
rv6 introduces the pair in the acknowledgment beat and pays it off at the 23% stat: some of that cohort are washers, a good number are probably wishers, and you cannot fix a sincere mistake with a policy aimed at liars. This is a genuine upgrade to the piece's taxonomy.
