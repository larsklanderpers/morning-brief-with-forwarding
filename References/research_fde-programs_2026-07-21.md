# Research Note — Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Programs Across the AI Labs
For: "Control" week / Monday "Fifty Agents" vendor-lanyard beat + Shape-of-Work FDE thread · Depth: deep · Date: 2026-07-21

## Bottom line (4 sentences)
The vendor-embedded-engineer model Lars flagged for Microsoft (~$2.5B) and AWS (~$1B) is not a hyperscaler quirk — it is now **table stakes for every Western frontier lab selling to enterprise.** OpenAI and Anthropic both went past "we're hiring FDEs" to standing up whole billion-dollar implementation vehicles in the last ten weeks: OpenAI's **Deployment Company** (11 May 2026, ~150 FDEs, >$4B) and Anthropic's **Ode with Anthropic** (unveiled 15 Jul 2026, Blackstone-backed). Google, Mistral, Cohere, Databricks, Meta and xAI all run or are standing up the same motion. The genuinely interesting contrast is China: the leading open-weight labs (Moonshot, DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM) mostly **do NOT** embed their own engineers — they commoditize the model with open weights and let cloud providers, "all-in-one" hardware makers, and system integrators own the last mile, with Zhipu the one partial exception.

**One-line thesis for a draft:** *The West defends closed-model margins by sending its own engineers into the customer; China commoditizes the model with open weights and lets cloud SIs and appliance vendors own the deployment — two opposite answers to the same "who implements this?" question.*

## The economics (the cost/margin story Lars leans on)
FDE is a deliberate **margin-for-moat** trade. Pure-software AI vendors chase 80%+ gross margins; going services-heavy (FDEs, solutions specialists) drags blended margin down but makes the vendor the customer's "system of work" — the a16z thesis. The labs concluded model quality no longer differentiates at the point of sale; **deployment does.** So they're racing to own the high-touch implementation layer before the consultancies (Accenture/Deloitte) or the hyperscalers (Microsoft ~$2.5B, AWS ~$1B) own the customer relationship. Ode's structure is the tell: putting the lower-margin services revenue in a **separately-branded, PE-backed vehicle** keeps it off Anthropic's own books while still capturing the deployment layer and PE-portfolio distribution. China runs the inverse economics: give the model away (open weights), let cloud providers/appliance makers/SIs monetize the last mile, and — via the "AI Plus" state mandate (>70% adoption by 2027) — fund integrator-led diffusion rather than lab-embedded engineers.
