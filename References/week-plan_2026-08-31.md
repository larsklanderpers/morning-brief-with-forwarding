That is three asks — management, shape of work, and attacker-speed-into-patch-management — and they

them as one.

label on them says.* Five weekdays produce the missing numbers and the decisions that follow.
Saturday gives the reader the one design choice that changes the outcome. Sunday names what is
underneath all of it: **the oversight everyone's governance model assumes is supplied by expertise
that the same organisations are quietly defunding, and no single one of them has an incentive to fund
it.**

**Registers, in order:** the gap (Mon) → the catch (Tue) → the recording (Wed) → the count (Thu) →
the invoice (Fri) → the design choice (Sat) → the tether (Sun).

Sat. **Shape of Defense** (proposed 14 Aug, never answered) carries Thu and Fri. The capstone's whole
move is that they are the same problem: the defender who cannot produce the severity number and the
