# Research note -- Tue 13 Oct 2026: AI-drafted performance reviews

Week plan: `week-plan_2026-10-12.md` (Tue 13 block, IDEA_BANK T3). Checked 9 Oct 2026 by a research subagent; primaries reached where marked SOLID.

## 1. Prevalence

- **ZeroBounce (SOLID primary, small manager base):** "Over 2 in 5 managers (41%) admit they have used AI to draft or revise a performance review." n=1,000 US professionals, of whom ONLY 250 managers. No method or dates. Released 8 Sep 2025. 26% of employees suspect their review was AI-generated. https://www.zerobounce.net/blog/newsroom/the-latest/ai-workplace-emails -- sells email validation; PR survey.
- **Omni Calculator (SOLID primary, best method):** "Which of these has your manager handled using AI instead of doing it themselves?" -- "Writing my performance review or feedback" 27%. n=913 employed US adults with a manager, Prolific, 8-15 Jun 2026, Census-balanced, MoE +/-3%. Also: 52% got feedback or a decision in the past year that "seemed to come from AI"; 68% say AI messages from a manager "feel less personal"; 42% say a manager who relies on AI "seems less competent". PERCEPTION, not behaviour. https://www.omnicalculator.com/reports/managers-using-ai -- digital-PR content, no HR product.
- **The Predictive Index (SOLID primary press release):** 44% of managers "admit to entering employee names or performance details into those tools" (public AI). 72% find public AI useful to prepare for people conversations. n=399 managers + 208 CEOs/leaders, July 2026, released 18 Aug 2026. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pi-study-finds-ceos-overestimate-manager-readiness-as-ai-changes-difficult-conversations-302853402.html -- PI sells a manager-development "behavioral intelligence platform" (adjacent interest; name it).
- **Resume Builder (CONTESTED):** percentages are of AI-USING managers only (78% raises, 77% promotions, 91% "assess performance"); n=1,342, Pollfish, Jun 2025. No review-writing figure. https://www.resumebuilder.com/half-of-managers-use-ai-to-determine-who-gets-promoted-and-fired/
- **Kickresume (weak):** 72% SUSPECT managers use AI for reviews; own user base, n=1,365, Nov 2025.
- **Headway 33% (UNVERIFIED, secondary only, TechRadar 1 Oct 2026).**
- **Gartner 8 Oct 2025 (SOLID, recommendation only):** names "drafting performance reviews" as a task CHROs could automate with managers; 8% of HR leaders think managers have the skills to use AI effectively. https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-10-08-gartner-research-finds-only-8-percent-of-hr-leaders-believe-their-managers-have-the-skills-to-effectively-use-ai
- Defensible range: roughly a quarter (employees' report, Omni) to two-fifths (managers' admission, ZeroBounce, n=250). Cite both with bases.

## 2. Quality and risk

- **Tong, Jia, Luo & Fang 2021, SMJ 42(9):1600-1631 (SOLID):** AI feedback improved performance (deployment); telling employees it was AI lowered performance (disclosure); weaker for longer-tenured staff. Analytics-system feedback, not LLM prose. https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/stratm/v42y2021i9p1600-1631.html
- **Cardon & Coman 2025, IJBC (SOLID):** n=1,100 professionals; at medium-high AI assistance readers "question the authorship, confidence, caring, sincerity, and ability" of the manager. UF release: 40-52% saw supervisor as sincere under high AI use vs 83% low. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/23294884251350599 ; https://news.ufl.edu/2025/08/writing-ai-work/
- **Reif, Larrick & Soll 2025, PNAS (SOLID):** social evaluation penalty for using AI; 4 experiments, N=4,439. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12088386/
- **Wan et al. 2023, Findings of EMNLP (SOLID, by analogy):** LLM reference letters show gender bias ("men being agentic ... women being communal"); "hallucination bias". Letters not reviews; 2023 models. https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.243/
- **Textio Jan 2023 (vendor, CONTESTED):** sells Textio Feedback.
- NOT FOUND: peer-reviewed evidence that LLMs invent specifics in reviews or worsen recency bias. Present as mechanism, not finding.

## 3. Regulation

- **EU AI Act Annex III 4(b) (SOLID):** "...to monitor and evaluate the performance and behaviour of persons in such relationships." https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/annex-3
- **Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 of 8 Jul 2026 (SOLID):** Annex III high-risk obligations now apply from 2 Dec 2027 (was 2 Aug 2026). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202601744
- Art. 6(3) intended-purpose test and carve-outs (CONTESTED interpretation): a manager pasting notes into a general chatbot is probably not the target; a dedicated scoring tool probably is.
- **NYC LL144 (SOLID):** "employment decision" = screen candidates for employment or employees for promotion. Does NOT cover appraisal.
