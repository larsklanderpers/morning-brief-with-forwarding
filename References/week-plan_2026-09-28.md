## Sun 4 Oct -- Capstone [not titled]

- **Merges:** the volume (Mon), the poisoned part (Wed), the platform on the desk (Thu), the small shop
  (Fri). Candidate finding: output got cheap; the parts, the platform and the checking did not.
- **Precis after research, 25 Sep (candidate, not ruled):** the unit cost of making fell on every leg (Warp PR ~$80 to ~$30; per-token cost to zero on the device; a product from one person), but the cost of a defect did not fall with it, and at volume it multiplies (Faros: incidents per PR up; one Brevo loader, every customer site, 4.5 hours). The scarce, priced step is the check per unit (Warp's own metric: human interactions per PR). Value concentrates with whoever owns a layer the platform cannot absorb (Base44 margin ~0% to ~60% on its own model; Stripe: top decile up, median down). ⚠️ Keep off "judgment is scarce" as the headline device (Aug week) and off Sun 27's frame entirely.
- ★ **HIS SPINE FOR SUN 4, 25 Sep, in his words -- this supersedes the candidate precis above where they differ:**
  - **The recall math.** It scales like car manufacturing, but *"a recall doesn't change the fact that the implication cost is much higher here as a function of the cost per unit. Most of these costs per unit are under $100, but they might execute 100,000 times."* Unit cost small, execution count huge, so the defect cost is the unit cost times the run count, not a line item.
  - **Judgment comes IN, but only as part of how it all fits together**, never as the headline device (his ruling overrides the earlier caution here; the Aug-week collision is on the device, not the theme).
  - **Craftsmanship vs. human-in-the-loop, his distinction:** *"craftsmanship is at the root of where you get the output without the incidents. Human-in-the-loop attention is where you get the incidents across the output."* Craft is upstream and prevents; attention is downstream and catches, after the volume has already multiplied the defect.
  - **Craftsmanship is becoming scarce**, and it is the thing a leader has to emphasize if they want their people to have it and their stuff to work -- *"not just speed... but the fact that problems at speed do nothing except accelerate the total impact of those problems."* That line is the tie.
  - **Footing it can use:** Warp ~$80 to ~$30 per PR and "human interactions per PR" (Mon); Faros incidents per PR up (Mon note); one Brevo loader, every site, 4.5 hours (Wed); unmetered per-token cost on the device removes the brake on run count (Thu); Base44 and Stripe on who keeps the margin (Fri).
