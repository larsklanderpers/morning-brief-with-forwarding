## The economics
- The two-month cap is the arithmetic of the seat. On a $20/user/month AI add-on for a 200-seat client, the partner pays roughly $4,000 a month through the distributor. That puts the distributor's ceiling to the partner at about **$8,000** (illustrative arithmetic on the s.15 wording, not a sourced figure).
- The partner's exposure to its customer is whatever its own MSA says, less whatever its own E&O will pay after its AI wording. The partner's side has no two-month ceiling unless it wrote one.
- The difference is the price of the seat. Nobody in public prices it, except as a vendor's "risk buffer".
- Adoption runs far ahead of revenue: 67% sell AI services, 13% make meaningful money from them (Kaseya). So most of the channel carries the risk on a product line that is not yet paying for it.
