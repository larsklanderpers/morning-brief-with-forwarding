something fun done with Astra or any other tool, written as *this is pretty cool* rather than as
analysis.

**What it is NOT.** *"Here's the news and here's what it means."* That is a weekday spine piece and
Wednesday to Friday already own it.

### ⛔ THE TUESDAY GATE

**Run at PLAN time. Every week. Written into the plan block, in these words, PASS or FAIL, with a
one-line reason.**

```
TUESDAY GATE: PASS|FAIL -- <one line>
```

**The test is not whether it is a good piece. It is whether it reads as a DIFFERENT KIND OF THING from
Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday.** If it does not seem different, it is not different.

**Automatic FAIL signals, any one of them:**
- Its thread string matches or nearly matches an adjacent weekday's.
- Its subject is a regulator, a vendor policy document, a breach, or a filing.
- It resolves to "here is the news and here is what it means."
- It is the third consecutive Tuesday on the same sub-thread.
- You cannot say in one line what a reader would DO differently on Tuesday afternoon.

**A FAIL is not a veto. It is a required call-out.** Write it down, say it to Lars, and re-pitch the
slot. A silent pass is the failure mode this gate exists to stop.

**Length is not the test.** Saturday is not measured in words, it is measured in whether it feels lighter
and whether a reader gains something. See the standing rule: lightness is a state of being, not a length
metric.

### ⛔ THE SATURDAY GATE

**Run at PLAN time. Every week. Written into the plan block, in these words, PASS or FAIL.**

```
SATURDAY GATE: PASS|FAIL -- human feature: <name it in one line, or say there is none>
```

**The test: can the block name ONE solid human feature or shared human feature** -- a family bot, a
control plane, a tool at a price, a thing he built, a thing a reader can have -- **in a single line?**

## 4. Length is guidance, never a rule -- HIS RULING, 10 Sep 2026

**Nothing in this file is a word count, and nothing in this file may become one.** His words:

> *"As much as I like guidance and sticking to guidance in terms of structure and organization... I have
> zero issue with running long if the topic is important enough. In my opinion, it's my structure, so
> there's nobody setting rules around what I can and can't do but me. If I feel like a piece is deserving
> of a longer read, then that piece is going to get a longer read. Guidance is guidance, but it is not a
> rule."*

- **The capstone can run 2,500 or 7,000 words.** He has no issue with Sunday becoming a genuine long-form
  piece where there is enough to say. **Sunday length is not a finding and is not to be flagged.**
- **Any piece can run long when the topic earns it.** Flag length so he can decide, offer the
  acknowledgment-aside as a third option alongside cut-or-ship, and then ship what he says.
- **The Saturday gate measures FEEL and READER GAIN, never length.** Lightness is a state of being, not a
  length metric. A 3,600-word Saturday is not a gate failure for being 3,600 words; the 5 Sep piece
  failed on being a body of studies, and it would have failed at half the length.
- **Every "norm" in the pipeline is a starting position, not a ceiling.** Where a plan, a skill or a panel
  seat treats a word count as a gate, that is the defect.

## 7. How this binds

1. **`CLAUDE.md` points here** under the week-plan standing rules, in eight lines and no more. ⚠️ **This
   is the only copy of these rules.** The first draft of that pointer restated the spine table and the
   fail signals, which is a second copy, which is the three-copies drift that has already cost this
   pipeline once. He caught it. Do not restate the charter in `CLAUDE.md`, in a week plan, or in a skill.
2. **Every week plan carries both gate lines**, in the Tuesday block and the Saturday block, PASS or
   FAIL with the reason. **A missing gate line is itself a FAIL** -- it means nobody ran it.
3. **The `day-start` skill checks for both lines at boot** and reports their absence as a finding, the
   same way it counts status boards. A day's chat cannot fix a bad plan, but it can refuse to draft to
   one silently.
4. **A gate FAIL never blocks Lars.** It is a call-out. He can overrule any of it in one word, and if he
   does, the plan records the overrule and the reason so it is not re-argued later.

5. ⛔ **ENFORCEMENT BEGINS WITH THE WEEK OF MONDAY 14 SEPTEMBER 2026. HIS RULING, 12 Sep 2026.**
   Verbatim: *"we only adopted the gate rule a couple of days ago because we kept missing the tuesday
   and saturday. enforcement needs to being Monday 14th."*
