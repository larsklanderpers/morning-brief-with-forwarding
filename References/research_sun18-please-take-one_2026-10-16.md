## Exhibits (footed)

1. **curl ends its bug bounty** -- Daniel Stenberg, 26 Jan 2026, https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/01/26/the-end-of-the-curl-bug-bounty/ (RE-OPENED)
   - "We no longer offer any monetary rewards for security reports – no matter which severity." Stops 31 Jan 2026.
   - "We saw an explosion in AI slop reports combined with a lower quality even in the reports that were not obvious slop"
   - "87 confirmed vulnerabilities and over 100,000 USD"; confirmed rate "somewhere north of 15%" in previous years, "below 5%" from 2025; "Not even one in twenty was real."
   - Considered charging reporters a fee; rejected (payments, chargebacks, excluding legitimate reporters). Lever: remove the prize.
   - Interest: none for curl; HackerOne loses a program.
2. **GitHub "Eternal September"** -- Ashley Wolf, 12 Feb 2026, https://github.blog/open-source/maintainers/welcome-to-the-eternal-september-of-open-source-heres-what-we-plan-to-do-for-maintainers/ (RE-OPENED)
   - "The cost to create has dropped but the cost to review has not." / "Today, a pull request can be generated in seconds."
   - Eternal September = Usenet 1993, new users every September, then AOL-era "the September that never ended".
3. **GitHub PR limits** -- changelog 17 Jun 2026 https://github.blog/changelog/2026-06-17-limit-open-pull-requests-for-users-without-write-access/ ; blog 18 Jun 2026 https://github.blog/open-source/maintainers/how-pull-request-limits-are-cutting-down-the-noise/ (RE-OPENED)
   - Caps open PRs for users without write access; bypass list for trusted contributors. No default number. No measured noise reduction published.
   - "In January 2023, developers merged about 25 million pull requests a month across GitHub. Today that number tops 90 million" (MERGED, not opened).
   - "Pull requests opened by Copilot or another AI agent will counts toward your limit." (sic) -- footed at primary.
   - Earlier: 13 Feb 2026 setting to disable PRs or restrict to collaborators.
   - Interest: GitHub sells Copilot AND the throttle. Name it.
4. **GitHub bug bounty** -- 15 May 2026, https://github.blog/security/raising-the-bar-quality-shared-responsibility-and-the-future-of-githubs-bug-bounty-program/ (subagent only)
   - "New tools, including AI, have lowered the barrier to entry for security research"; working proof of concept now required; low-impact valid findings get swag, not cash. Post welcomes AI tools. Lever: move the gate to proof.
5. **Cloudflare: counter-model first, then re-price** (RE-OPENED)
   - AI Labyrinth, 19 Mar 2025, https://blog.cloudflare.com/ai-labyrinth/ : "uses AI-generated content to slow down, confuse, and waste the resources of AI Crawlers"; "more than 50 billion requests to the Cloudflare network every day ... just under 1% of all web requests"; "No real human would go four links deep into a maze of AI-generated nonsense."
   - Pay per crawl, 1 Jul 2025, https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-pay-per-crawl/ : "Cloudflare returns an HTTP 402 Payment Required response, accompanied by a crawler-price header." Options Allow / Charge ("the configured, domain-wide price") / Block. Same day: new domains asked whether to allow AI crawlers (press release).
   - Interest: large. Cloudflare sells the gate and wants to be the marketplace.
