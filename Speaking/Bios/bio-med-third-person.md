Lars Klander has spent thirty years building software for environments where the rules are not optional: gaming, banking and defense. As an independent software vendor he delivered gaming management infrastructure to four of the five largest gaming companies in the world, and software he helped create runs in more than 150 gaming venues across the United States, Australia and Macau. He has written numerous programming books, translated into more than 25 languages, and a network and cyber security book that went through two editions.

For the last decade he has been CTO of Player Elite in Sydney, where he builds and operates AI agents inside a regulated business, and writes daily on AI governance and control design.
