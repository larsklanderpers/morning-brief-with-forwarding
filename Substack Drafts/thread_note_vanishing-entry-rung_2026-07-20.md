## Core argument (the article's spine)

Companies are automating the entry-level rung and, in the same breath, discovering they've cut the pipeline that manufactures their own senior talent. The tasks a junior does — first-draft, reconcile, summarise — are exactly what AI does well now, so the 22–25 cohort is being thinned. But the *tacit* half of a junior job (the shortcuts, the judgment, the "I've seen this go wrong before") is where managers actually come from, and it only accrues by doing the work. Kill the rung and you save a little now and lose your future managers — and your early-warning system — later. The resolution isn't "don't automate," it's "redesign the role": move the junior from performing the task to **checking whether the machine did it right**.

## The stats (all [VERIFY] — Quartz's characterisations; confirm against primaries before any draft)

- **[VERIFY]** Matt Garman (AWS) called replacing junior devs with AI "one of the dumbest things I've ever heard"; AWS hiring ~11,000 interns/recent grads this year after cutting ~14,000 corporate jobs last fall.
- **[VERIFY]** Erik Brynjolfsson (Stanford Digital Economy Lab) + ADP payroll records: workers aged 22–25 in the most AI-exposed jobs lost **16%** of employment relative to older colleagues doing the same work; erosion running since 2022.
- **[VERIFY]** Software developers 22–25 lost **~a fifth** of their jobs vs the late-2022 peak while developers 35+ kept growing through the same stretch. Young marketing/sales managers down but less; stock clerks flat regardless of age; young health aides *gained* faster than older peers.
- **[VERIFY]** Brynjolfsson + ADP Research live dashboard through April: decline for the exposed 22–25 group sharpened to **~4%/yr**, up from **<3%** after the first 12 months; three-plus years pointing one direction.
- **[VERIFY]** Amy Edmondson (HBS) + Tomas Chamorro-Premuzic argue cutting these roles trades a small immediate saving for the manager-pipeline; accounting firms cited as the redesign template (AI clears routine matching/reporting; juniors move to chasing anomalies and client conversations).
