## 3. Nadella — "You Pay for Intelligence Twice" — HOLD, solid perspective
**Thread:** AI Sovereignty / Trust Boundaries (he literally uses the term "trust boundary")
**Primary:** Blog post published via X (@satyanadella), Sunday 12 Jul — the "Reverse Information Paradox."
**Coverage:**
- [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/13/satya-nadella-has-issued-a-shocking-warning-to-companies-using-ai/)
- [The New Stack — best analysis](https://thenewstack.io/nadella-reverse-information-paradox/)
- [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/you-pay-for-ai-twice-satya-nadellas-reverse-information-paradox-raises-a-billion-dollar-question-542430-2026-07-12)
**Key lines:** Models learn from "exhaust" — prompts, tool use, and especially corrections; every correction distills institutional know-how to the provider. Asymmetry compounds: provider learns about customer continuously, customer learns nothing about what's done with it. His fix: a **"trust boundary" — a line across which nothing passes without the company's consent**; inside it, data, traces, evals, adapted weights, and organizational memory accumulate together.
**Note:** Microsoft's CEO independently arrived at Lars's week-of-13-Jul framing and named it "trust boundary." That's quotable validation for this week AND a load-bearing piece of next week's Control theme.
