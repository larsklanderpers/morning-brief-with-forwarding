## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

**Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE):** The clearest breakout role of 2026. 5,330 job postings as of April 2026 — a **729% year-over-year increase**. Salary range $170K–$200K+. The role sits at the intersection of sales, deployment, and technical ownership: FDEs turn AI demos into governed production systems. Core skills in 2026 are agentic orchestration (LangGraph, CrewAI), evaluation frameworks, and AI observability/guardrails — on top of RAG and fine-tuning fundamentals.
*Archetype mapping:* **Builder + Orchestrator.** This is the "become the maker for your function" archetype crystallised into a job title and a salary band.
Source: [Medium/Generative AI Revolution](https://medium.com/generative-ai-revolution-ai-native-transformation/forward-deployed-engineer-the-new-200k-ai-role-built-for-the-enterprise-ai-adoption-era-659eeadd0a6f)

**AI FinOps Specialist:** Formally recognised as a FinOps Framework category since March 19, 2026. The job is watching the "Token Tax" — AI usage costs that grow invisibly until cloud bills explode. Verified salary postings: Qualcomm $155K–$233K, Singtel, Autodesk.
*Archetype mapping:* **Risk Steward.** The person who keeps the Jevons dynamic from quietly bankrupting the function.

**AI Quality Assurance Tester / Algorithm Auditor:** Rising demand driven by the EU AI Act compliance wave. The job is making AI systems safe, unbiased, and auditable — test design for non-deterministic systems is the genuinely hard skill.
*Archetype mapping:* **Risk Steward + Editor.** Editing AI output at system level, not sentence level.

**Agent Orchestrator (as baseline, not title):** The community conversation this week is consistent — orchestration is becoming standard-issue, like spreadsheet literacy. The question is no longer "who is the orchestration specialist" but "what does orchestration look like for someone in marketing / legal / ops."

## 📌 Today's Signal

The BLS revision landing today is the most concrete data point this community thread has seen in weeks — and it cuts both ways. The job totals were weaker than reported, which feeds the displacement concern; but the Yale Budget Lab's concurrent finding (no occupational-mix shift) suggests the disruption is sector-level and role-level, not yet macroeconomic. That gap — between what's happening inside tech and what the headline numbers show — is exactly where the architecture-of-work frame becomes useful. The Forward Deployed Engineer's 729% posting surge and the AI FinOps Specialist's FinOps Foundation recognition aren't in the BLS data yet; new archetypes don't appear in the taxonomy until the taxonomy is rebuilt. The community's job isn't to wait for the BLS to catch up. It's to name the roles before the recruiters do.
