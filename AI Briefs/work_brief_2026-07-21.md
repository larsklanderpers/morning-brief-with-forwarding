## 🎥 What Creators Are Saying (YouTube)

**"The Job Positions of the AI Future" — The AI Daily Brief / NLW** (Sunday 5 July 2026) · https://aidailybrief.ai/e/2026-07-05

This is still the foundational episode for this thread — and the clearest frame available. NLW builds from a Boris Cherny tweet sketching five product-facing archetypes (Prototyper, Builder, Sweeper, Grower, Maintainer) and argues Cherny missed the outward-facing half of the org: Editor, Scout, Evangelist, Orchestrator, Conductor, Risk Steward. The meta-shift: people are no longer doing the job — they're managing agents that do it, and figuring out what substance remains. The one practical move: become the Maker for your function.

💡 Constructive read: The archetype lens cuts through the noise in a way job titles can't. When "what does an AI do to my job?" becomes "which archetype am I?", you stop being the object of disruption and start being the one choosing your position in the new org chart.

Key frames from the episode:
- The Prototyper eliminates the "endless discussion phase" — prototypes are now the first conversation
- The Risk Steward is an **accelerator**, not a bottleneck — anticipates what could derail a project several steps ahead and removes it before the project gets there
- Back-office roles (finance, ops, HR) concentrate on Maintainer, Sweeper, Risk Steward, and Orchestrator — the "back office is already the maintainer of the org's core functions"
- Scout is the first archetype that will routinely be filled by an agent (aggregating and distilling incoming signal)
- Every function is now growing a Maker — a back-office person can build custom software for a specific expense-reporting exception instead of filing a ticket and waiting a quarter

## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

**Forward-Deployed Engineer (FDE)** — The most concrete crystallization of the "become the maker for your function" archetype today. 224 open roles at 39 AI companies; postings grew 800% in 2025; median mid-level comp is $385K, staff-level $610K, principal FDEs at frontier labs clearing $1.2M. OpenAI launched "The Deployment Company" (May 2026, $4B+), Anthropic announced a $1.5B joint venture with Blackstone and Goldman Sachs to embed Claude FDEs inside financial-services customers. *Architecture-of-work lens: FDE is the Builder + Orchestrator archetypes fused into a client-facing role — builds and tunes agentic workflows inside the customer's own stack.* Source: [Paraform](https://www.paraform.com/blog/forward-deployed-ai-engineer), [jobsbyculture.com](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/forward-deployed-engineer-boom-2026)

**AI FinOps Specialist** — Manages the financial side of AI infrastructure; answers the question "is the AI actually making money or just consuming resources?" The "Token Tax" problem (rising cloud bills from unaudited AI usage) is making this role urgent inside any org that's deployed more than a handful of agent workflows. *Architecture-of-work lens: Risk Steward + Sweeper.* Source: [FinOps Foundation](https://www.finops.org/insights/ai-for-finops-agentic-use-cases/)

**Algorithm Auditor** — Quality-control and compliance role for AI decisions; required for high-risk AI systems under EU AI Act (rolling out 2024-26). Growing fast as regulatory pressure creates demand. *Architecture-of-work lens: Risk Steward, with heavy Maintainer overlap.* Source: [PrometAI](https://prometai.app/blog/new-jobs-created-by-ai)

**Agent Supervisor / AI Orchestrator** — Designs and manages multi-agent systems; keeps outputs coherent as agent output surges. Distinct from the general "orchestration as baseline skill everyone acquires" — this is the dedicated organizational layer in larger orgs. Agentic AI job postings broadly grew 280% YoY, ~90,000 US listings. *Architecture-of-work lens: Conductor + Orchestrator as dedicated roles, not just archetypes.* Source: [Forbes/Sandy Carter](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sandycarter/2026/06/02/the-20-new-agentic-ai-jobs-box-mckinsey-and-linkedin-all-see-coming/)

**Workers with advanced AI skills earn 56% more** than peers in the same roles without those skills (2026 data). The skill premium is no longer about getting into AI — it's about being fluent in AI *inside your existing role*. Source: [Medium/Technology Hits](https://medium.com/technology-hits/future-job-roles-in-ai-2026-what-skills-will-you-need-4e376d29cb3f)

## ⚖️ The Debate (kept honest, kept brief)

Altman and Amodei both walked back "jobs apocalypse" predictions in May 2026. Altman told Commonwealth Bank's CEO he was wrong: "I thought there would have been more impact on entry-level white-collar jobs being eliminated by now than has actually happened." Yale Budget Lab: "no meaningful change in unemployment rates for AI-exposed workers" since ChatGPT launch. BCG's read: AI will *reshape* more jobs than it replaces — new tasks added to AI-exposed roles are 2.5x more likely to require empathy, judgment, and creativity. Counter-signal: Tech layoffs through May 2026 have hit 115,000 (approaching all-2025's 124,000), with Meta, Amazon, and Snap explicitly citing AI. The soft landing is real — but it's not evenly distributed. Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/26/sam-altman-dario-amodei-walking-back-ai-jobs-apocalypse-prophecies-ipo/), [Forbes/Markman](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/05/29/the-job-apocalypse-sam-altman-and-dario-amodei-warned-about-never-came/), [BCG](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/ai-will-reshape-more-jobs-than-it-replaces)

## 📌 Today's Signal

The community conversation has quietly crossed a threshold: the question is no longer "will AI take jobs?" — both the CEOs building the AI and the labor economists tracking the data have stopped saying it will, at least on the catastrophic timeline. The question now is **what you build into your role before someone else builds it for you**. The FDE salary data makes this vivid: the people who took "become the maker for your function" literally — who embedded inside a customer or a department and wired AI into that stack — are now the best-compensated knowledge workers in the economy. The archetype framework from NLW's July 5 episode gives everyone a map for doing a version of that inside any function. The Maker move isn't a career change; it's a disposition you add to the role you already have.
