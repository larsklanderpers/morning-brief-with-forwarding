## 🧑‍💻 Practitioner Ground Truth (Reddit)

*Reddit JSON endpoints were blocked by network policy this session. Fell back to web signal for practitioner perspective.*

The practitioner picture from web sources this week: entry-level job postings are down 35% over 18 months, with the sharpest drops in writing, coding, customer service, and analysis. Unemployment among 22–27-year-old college graduates hit 5.6% in March — one of the highest rates in a decade. Workers in the most AI-exposed occupations saw a 16% relative employment decline. MIT Technology Review's May headline: "It's time to address the looming crisis in entry-level work."

The nuance practitioners are actually navigating: 85% of graduating seniors now use AI tools (up 31 percentage points in two years), and AI-skilled workers earn 28–56% more than peers. The entry ladder is being pulled up — but the platform at the top is bigger than it's ever been.

## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

**Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** — The hottest AI job title in the US right now, by some measures. 224 open roles across 39 AI companies; postings up sharply from April 2025 to April 2026. Base: $215K–$310K; total comp at frontier labs clears $500K. Hiring: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Cloud, Stripe. What they do: embed at customer sites, learn workflows in painful detail, ship custom code that makes the product actually work for that specific context. · [forwardfuture.ai](https://briefing.forwardfuture.ai/) · [kore1.com](https://www.kore1.com/hire-ai-forward-deployed-engineers-2026/)
→ **Architecture-of-work mapping:** This is the Builder + Scout hybrid in the NLW/Cherny taxonomy — someone who builds *and* surfaces signal from the field. High durability because it requires contextual trust that an agent can't yet self-authorize.

**Agent Orchestration Specialist** — 739 job listings in February 2026, a 1,294% YoY increase. The Eightfold AI framing: "the most important job of 2026." Orchestrators don't just configure agents; they architect integrated human-AI teams and manage them like a workforce. · [eightfold.ai/blog/most-important-job-2026](https://eightfold.ai/blog/most-important-job-2026)
→ **Architecture-of-work mapping:** This is the Orchestrator archetype becoming formalized. It's converging with Aaron Levie's "agent deployer and manager" JD from the Box perspective.

**Agent Deployer & Manager** (emerging enterprise title, per @levie) — The team-level version of orchestration. Not a C-suite hire — this is the person on your team who figures out which of your current workflows can be handed to agents and ships the transition. Requires process mapping + technical comfort with MCP, evals, and CLI tooling.
→ **Architecture-of-work mapping:** Conductor-level thinking at the team scale. This is the role most knowledge workers are implicitly being asked to grow into — the question is whether organizations name it or leave it formless.

**AI FinOps / Model Cost Manager** — Emerging alongside the inference cost explosion. Organizations running multi-agent pipelines are discovering the need for someone who tracks and optimizes model spend the way cloud FinOps teams track compute. Not yet a formalized title but showing up in job descriptions.
→ **Architecture-of-work mapping:** Risk Steward archetype, financial lens. Natural home for anyone with a background in cloud infrastructure economics.

## ⚖️ The Debate (kept honest, kept brief)

The predicted jobs apocalypse did not arrive on schedule. Altman and Amodei have both walked back earlier predictions (Amodei's 2025 "50% of white-collar jobs gone in 5 years" claim; Altman's tech displacement warnings). The Yale Budget Lab found no meaningful change in unemployment rates for AI-exposed workers since ChatGPT launched. WEF's 2026 projection: 170 million new roles created by 2030 vs. 92 million displaced — net gain of 78 million.

The real pressure point is entry-level: 35% drop in postings over 18 months, meaningful uptick in recent-graduate unemployment, and a genuine question about how the next generation builds the baseline experience that historically feeds senior roles. That problem is real and unresolved; the macro narrative of net job gain doesn't help a 24-year-old whose expected on-ramp has narrowed.

## 📌 Today's Signal

The Replit "self-driving company" data — 2.9x output per engineer, flat quality metrics, agents doing PR review and incident response — is the week's clearest signal that the architecture-of-work shift is empirical, not theoretical. The community conversation is catching up: the debate is no longer *will* this happen but *what does it look like to navigate it well*. The roles crystallizing right now (FDE, Agent Orchestrator, Agent Deployer) all share the same shape — a human who understands the domain, manages the agents that execute within it, and holds accountability for output quality. That's not a narrow technical role; it's the old definition of "senior professional" with a new set of tools. The people who thrive are the ones who recognize they're already most of the way there.
