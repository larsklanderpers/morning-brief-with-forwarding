## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

**The employee-maker at scale: 4,000 builders inside a telco, and the wall they hit**
Source: AI Chat Daily | [Link](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-careers/sk-telecom-says-7-500-employees-now-use)
SK Telecom said on Sunday that more than 7,500 staff use agents monthly and over 4,000 have built their own, after a March push to build tools for their own jobs, with about 1,500 agents in regular use, per AI Chat Daily's account of The Korea Herald, which was not found or read here. AI Board head Kim In-soo says most problems individuals could solve alone are solved, and the harder ones need company support: "You identify a task where AI could be highly effective, only to discover that the data it needs cannot be provided." The company gave no savings or productivity figures, and it sells AI services. Its agent ID scheme ran here on 26 Oct; these adoption numbers are the new part.
💡 Constructive read: Give everyone a shovel and the garden gets dug fast, until someone hits the water main. This is "become the maker" at company scale and its real next step: the Builder archetype gets you to the wall, and the next job is the Conductor who can get the gate opened.

**The data translator: writing down what the fields mean**
Source: AI Chat Daily | [Link](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-careers/sk-telecom-says-7-500-employees-now-use)
In one project across about 9,000 databases and 300,000 data fields, SK Telecom used AI to write plain-language explanations of technical labels, and says answer accuracy rose from around 40% to over 80%. The figures are the company's own, as relayed. No such job title was reported; the role label is this brief's inference.
💡 Constructive read: A library without a catalogue is a warehouse. Through the architecture-of-work lens this is unglamorous Maintainer work with an outsized payoff, and anyone who can explain what the columns in their department's system really mean holds the cheapest upgrade available.

**The agent portfolio reviewer: counting results, not agents**
Source: AI Chat Daily | [Link](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-careers/sk-telecom-says-7-500-employees-now-use)
SK Telecom is reviewing which agents justify their operating costs. "Our goal isn't simply to reduce the number of agents. It's to produce results," Kim said. With over 4,000 builders and about 1,500 agents in regular use, the sorting is already under way. The company gives no retirement count, and the role label is this brief's inference.
💡 Constructive read: Orchards need a pruner as much as a planter. This is the Sweeper with a budget line: list the agents your team runs, what each costs and what it delivered, and you own a review nobody has asked for yet.

**The legacy migrator: see X above**
Source: X | [Link](https://x.com/emollick/status/2116526543346483419)
If Mollick's backlog is real, the role is an old one made affordable again: scoping and shepherding moves off legacy systems. A reply on the thread adds a second option, modernising the old toolchain instead of moving off it. No hiring data was seen.
💡 Constructive read: This is the Prototyper and Maintainer in one seat, and it flips the usual career advice: deep knowledge of the unfashionable system may be the best ticket in the building.

## ⚖️ The Debate (kept honest, kept brief)

**Goldman: workers displaced from AI-exposed jobs take about a month longer to find work**
Source: AI Chat Daily | [Link](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-careers/goldman-sachs-finds-ai-displaced-workers-take-month-longer)
AI Chat Daily reports a Goldman note by Pierfrancesco Mei, released 29 Oct: those displaced since 2025 took about a month longer than other displaced workers and were roughly 10 percentage points likelier to be unemployed a year on. "AI-displaced" is Goldman's own proxy across more than 15,000 workers, since no government survey asks. Read only through this outlet, and distinct from Goldman's April note, which gave a similar one-month figure for technology displacement. Goldman's history shows younger, college-educated, urban and shorter-tenure workers recover best.
💡 Constructive read: Scar tissue forms where a tool fits only one bench. The note's most useful line is why short-tenure workers do better: their skills travel, so ask this week which of yours would still work in a different company.

**Comcast cuts 912 and plans over 1,100 new positions, with AI as one reason among several**
Source: AI Chat Daily | [Link](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-careers/comcast-cuts-912-jobs-three-states-expands-ai)
Per a Pennsylvania WARN notice as reported, 634 Philadelphia roles go on 31 Dec, plus 119 in New Jersey and 159 in Washington, expected to include engineers, product managers, analysts and IT leaders; the 1,100-plus new roles include over 500 in Philadelphia, with no breakdown of what they are. Executives said on the earnings call they plan to deploy AI agents with customers, but the CFO framed it as simplifying the organisation, and Comcast lost 144,000 broadband customers in Q3 and is splitting off NBCUniversal and Sky. The headline's AI link is the outlet's framing. A web search did not independently confirm the notice.
💡 Constructive read: When a ship is being refitted, a new engine is rarely the only reason the crew changes. Read past the headline to the job list: the same reorganisation that removes one set of roles is creating another, and those new roles are the part worth asking about.

**Challenger's count and a quiet calendar**
Source: AI Chat Daily | [Link](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-careers/goldman-sachs-finds-ai-displaced-workers-take-month-longer)
The same article cites Challenger's 120,136 announced cuts citing AI through September, about 21% of all cuts and the most common stated reason; that is what employers say, not a measured cause, and Challenger's report was not read here. It also sets Stanford's roughly 19% relative employment gap for 22 to 25 year-olds in exposed jobs against Yale Budget Lab's finding of no link to employment or unemployment changes. Proton's re-interviews begin Wednesday (4 to 11 Nov) and Microsoft's exit decisions are due in early December; neither has moved.
💡 Constructive read: A stated reason is a label on the box, not an inventory of what is inside. Keep the announcement counts and the labour data in separate columns and you will not be whipsawed by either.

## 📌 Today's Signal

All week the question has been how to stay in charge of the work, and today it runs into the plumbing. SK Telecom shows what "become the maker" looks like after the first flush of success: thousands of employees built their own agents, the easy problems are gone, and the next ones need data, permissions and someone willing to prune. That makes the next role less heroic and more useful: the person who writes down what the fields mean, gets the gate opened and asks which agents earned their keep. Medin's habit keeps you in the decision, Mollick's backlog says deep knowledge of old systems may be worth more than it was a year ago, and Goldman's note, read here second-hand, says skills that travel are the ones that help after a shock. Treat every number today as self-reported or relayed. Through the architecture-of-work lens, the Builder gets you started and the Conductor gets you past the wall. The Sunday action: pick the thing you built or wanted to build with AI that stalled, write down exactly which data or permission stopped it and who owns that gate, and send them one line this week.
