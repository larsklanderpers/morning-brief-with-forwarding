## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

**Forward-Deployed Engineer / Forward-Deployed Orchestrator (FDO)**
The biggest concrete new-role signal of the week. The FDE role (builds and integrates the agent) has spawned a partner: the **FDO** (Forward Deployed Orchestrator) — a process + change expert embedded in a client's live operation, accountable for adoption and continuous optimization of the combined human-and-agent workforce. Job postings for FDEs alone up 729% YoY. Salary: $170k–$200k+.

*Architecture of work lens:* FDE = **Builder** archetype. FDO = **Conductor** archetype — the person who keeps the human-agent orchestra in tune on the ground. This is the first time we're seeing Conductor as a distinct job title in the market.

**AI FinOps Specialist**
Fast-growing finance-adjacent role focused on whether AI systems are actually generating value vs. consuming resources. NLW's Sep 25 episode on Jev use cases touched this directly — the "is it making money or just burning tokens?" question is now someone's full-time job at larger orgs.

*Architecture of work lens:* **Risk Steward** — the person who owns the accountability layer for AI spend.

**"Maker" role (Microsoft framing)**
Microsoft's Agent-a-Thon in Sep 2026 formalized a "Maker" tier — participants who use Copilot Studio to automate work and create scalable no-code agents that solve real challenges. Microsoft is now using "Maker" as an internal role designation, not just an analogy.

*Architecture of work lens:* Confirms the "become the maker for your function" thesis is moving from NLW's framing into corporate HR vocabulary.

**AI Judgment/Question Writer (emergent)**
Jev's probabilistic model introduces a new skill: writing questions the model can answer with calibrated confidence. As judgment-layer models enter business software, the person who writes the questions becomes structurally important — similar to how the prompt engineer role emerged, but with more rigor around verifiability.

*Architecture of work lens:* Sits between **Editor** (quality gate on AI output) and **Scout** (hunting for where AI can be applied).
