## ⚖️ The Debate (kept honest, kept brief)

The apocalypse predictions haven't landed — yet. The Yale Budget Lab found no significant differences in occupation mix or unemployment duration for high-AI-exposure workers through late 2025. Goldman Sachs' latest AI Adoption Tracker (May 2026) puts net job losses at ~11K/month — 30% below their own earlier estimates. Both Altman and Amodei have publicly walked back their most alarming job-displacement predictions from early 2026. Deutsche Bank flagged "AI redundancy washing" as a feature of 2026: companies attributing layoffs to AI that have other causes.

That said: the bottom of the career ladder is compressing. Entry-level analyst, junior dev, first-year associate — these roles are genuinely narrowing. The WEF projects 170M new roles by 2030 against 92M displaced (net +78M), but the new roles require different skills than the displaced ones. The gap between the top AI users and average ones has grown from 2.6x to 8.3x productivity differential (OpenAI internal data, Aug 2026 AIDB).

Sources: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/ai-roiling-job-market-data-202623765.html · https://memeburn.com/sam-altman-and-dario-amodei-ai-jobs-apocalypse-walkback-2026/
