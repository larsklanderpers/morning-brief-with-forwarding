## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

**The silent-error checker**
Source: CEPR VoxEU | [Link](https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/letting-ai-agents-write-econometric-code)
The CEPR benchmark finds that the failures left under the agent setup are scripts that run cleanly but return wrong numbers, and says catching them is still the researcher's work. It also reports that the agent setup costs more to run than a single chatbot pass.
💡 Constructive read: This is the Editor archetype with a sharper brief. When the loud errors disappear, what is left is the quiet error, and the person who can spot a plausible wrong answer becomes the bottleneck worth paying for. That is a learnable habit (sanity checks, known-answer tests, a second method) rather than a gift.

**An AI-first studio: Epic's Disney shooter**
Source: AI Chat Daily, citing Bloomberg | [Link](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-business/epic-games-goes-ai-first-unannounced-disney-shooter-delays)
The AI brief covered this on 14 Oct; the roles angle is new here. Bloomberg reported on 13 Oct that a game director on Epic's unannounced Disney shooter told staff there would be "no more hand-written code" and that the game's art would be generated with full AI workflows, with Anthropic's Claude as a primary tool. Epic confirmed it in part through a spokesperson. The project has slipped past its March window.
💡 Constructive read: A production that mandates generated code and art does not empty the studio. It turns the developers into Builders who specify, Maintainers who keep the pipeline running and Editors who decide what is good enough to ship. The slip is the honest part of the story: changing how the work is organised costs time before it saves any.

**Forward-deployed engineer keeps spreading beyond the labs**
Source: ServiceNow Careers | [Link](https://careers.servicenow.com/jobs/744000154008689/principal-ai-forward-deployed-engineer/)
ServiceNow is advertising a Principal AI Forward Deployed Engineer role, and a search summary of current listings describes the job as scoping the problem with the customer, building in the customer's own stack, writing the evals and staying until people use the system daily. Listings are not a measure of hiring volume, and "agent orchestrator" as a title appears far less often than FDE or AI agent engineer.
💡 Constructive read: The FDE is the Prototyper and the Grower in one person, sent to sit with the customer. For anyone who knows a domain well and can build a little, it is one of the clearest entry points into the agent economy, because the domain knowledge is the scarce half.

## 📌 Today's Signal

Launch day for Co-Existence gives the feed its theme: the human is moving out of the middle of the loop and to its ends. Mollick says the era of checking every step is closing. His STATA post and the CEPR benchmark show what that looks like in practice: agents that run their own code get most answers right, and what is left is the quiet error that only someone who knows the field will catch. Epic's AI-first mandate shows the same shift at studio scale, and the delay shows the reorganisation cost that comes with it. The archetypes that grow are the Editor who catches the plausible wrong answer, the Builder who specifies what to make, and the forward-deployed Prototyper who brings domain knowledge to the customer. The practical move this week is to pick one piece of your own work an agent could now do end to end, and write down how you would know if its answer was quietly wrong. That checklist is the skill that is gaining value.
