## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

**Forward Deployed Engineer** — the biggest signal this week
· Job postings jumped 729% YoY: 643 open roles in April 2025 → 5,330 in April 2026. Salary range: $170K–$200K+.
· What the role is: embeds with customers to build and tune agentic workflows inside their specific tech stack. The closest analogy is the consulting engineer + product manager in one body.
· Architecture-of-work mapping: **Builder + Scout + Orchestrator** — they prototype inside the client's env, scout what the agents can handle, and orchestrate the handoff to production.
· Source: https://medium.com/generative-ai-revolution-ai-native-transformation/forward-deployed-engineer-the-new-200k-ai-role-built-for-the-enterprise-ai-adoption-era-659eeadd0a6f

**Algorithm Auditor**
· AI quality-control specialist: drift detection, hallucination loops, compliance certification proving AI decisions are traceable and fair. Procurement mandates in regulated industries now require this by contract.
· Architecture-of-work mapping: **Risk Steward** — the named institutional role for the archetype the whole framework implies but rarely makes explicit.

**Agent Architect**
· Designs multi-agent systems: orchestration patterns (planner-executor, supervisor-worker), state management, safety triggers, escalation points. Demand is concentrated in enterprises that have accumulated AI prototypes over the last two years and now need someone to turn them into governed production systems.
· Architecture-of-work mapping: **Builder + Conductor** — the person who decides how the orchestra is seated before anyone starts playing.

**AgentOps Engineer / Agent Manager**
· The operations layer for agent systems — monitoring, reliability, cost control (the AI FinOps function is folding into this), and the human-in-the-loop escalation design.
· Architecture-of-work mapping: **Maintainer + Sweeper** — keep the system clean, running, and within budget.

**Wage premium signal:** Workers with AI skills commanding 56% wage premium over peers (WEF data). The premium is highest in roles that cross technical and managerial — the Orchestrator and Conductor archetypes.

## ⚖️ The Debate (kept honest, kept brief)

Altman and Amodei are both walking back their jobs-apocalypse predictions heading into their respective IPO windows (Fortune, May 2026). a16z calls AI displacement fears "unhelpful marketing, bad economics and worse history." The Yale Budget Lab finds no meaningful change in unemployment for AI-exposed roles since ChatGPT's launch. The BLS August 28 benchmark revision landed small (−79K annual jobs vs −911K the prior year) — and crucially, the forecast error (~262K) exceeds twice the total AI-attributed layoff count, meaning we're still measuring with a ruler too blunt for the signal.

The nuance: tech entry-level hiring is down 30–50% at major firms vs. 2023, and global tech layoffs hit 115K+ by May 2026. The disruption is real and concentrated — it's just not the mass-unemployment event the headline models predicted. The honest frame: the composition of work is changing faster than the count of jobs.

## 📌 Today's Signal

The community conversation this week has moved past "will AI take jobs" and landed on "what are the actual new jobs and what do they pay." The Forward Deployed Engineer number — 729% YoY growth in open postings — is the most concrete data point in the brief: it names a role, attaches a salary, and shows the growth curve. Paired with Mollick's framing that the bottleneck is now organizational coordination (not model capability), today's signal is that the adaptation path is clearer than the anxiety suggests. The workers who will capture the 56% wage premium are the ones treating the Boris Cherny archetypes not as job descriptions but as a self-audit tool: which of these am I already doing? Which cluster of tasks maps to my current role? The maker for your function is not a new hire — it's whoever in the function decides to become it first.
