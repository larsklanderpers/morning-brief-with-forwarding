## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

### Fastest-moving title: Forward Deployed Engineer
Job postings jumped **729% YoY** (643 April 2025 → 5,330 April 2026). OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, EY all hiring. Salary: $170k–$200k+. The role: embed inside a customer, build and tune agentic workflows in the real environment. Bottleneck in enterprise AI has moved from model access to deployment capability.
- 💡 *Architecture lens:* Forward Deployed Engineer = Prototyper + Orchestrator operating at a customer's site rather than a lab. The role exists because building in a demo environment and deploying in a real one are different jobs.

### AI FinOps Specialist
Emerging fast as agents scale. Manages token budgets, decides when to use frontier models vs. cheaper models, tracks cost per accepted task. 20–35% of enterprise AI operational cost is now orchestration compute.
- 💡 *Architecture lens:* This is the Sweeper applied to the AI cost stack — the person who prunes what has quietly stopped being worth it.

### Algorithm Auditor / AI Governance Specialist
Procurement mandates now frequently require bias testing and AI audits. Growing demand with public policy backgrounds.
- 💡 *Architecture lens:* Risk Steward operating at procurement rather than product — shifts from reactive gating to anticipatory governance.

### Work Architect / AI Steward (WEF framing)
World Economic Forum's June 2026 language for roles that design and oversee automation systems. Not yet a common job title but appearing in early postings.
- 💡 *Architecture lens:* The Conductor operating at org design level — ensuring the agent fleet outputs stay coherent with business intent.

### AI Agent Orchestration Specialist
Eightfold called this "the most important job of 2026." Coordinates multi-agent pipelines, manages task distribution, ensures coherence.
- 💡 *Architecture lens:* This IS the Orchestrator archetype — the first role that makes the AIDB Jul 5 framework explicit as a job title.

## ⚖️ The Debate (kept honest, kept brief)

Amodei and Altman both walked back apocalyptic predictions in May 2026. Amodei — who predicted 50% white-collar job loss — now cites Jevons paradox: automation may expand the work people do, not shrink it. Altman said those predictions were too extreme. The Yale Budget Lab through mid-2026 found no significant changes in occupational mix or unemployment duration in high-AI-exposure jobs since ChatGPT's 2022 launch. Early 2026 employment data shows stability as AI boosts productivity rather than replacing headcount at scale. The debate is real; the apocalypse is not on schedule.

## 📌 Today's Signal

The conversation has quietly moved past "will AI take jobs" and into "what does good human judgment look like inside an AI-augmented workflow." The concrete answer emerging from every lane today: **the person who knows when to trust the output, when to push back, and how to read the bill.** Token literacy, quality judgment, and the ability to define a task crisply are not soft skills — they are the three technical skills of the agentic era. The archetype-of-work frame isn't a prediction about the distant future; it's a vocabulary for what Frontier Professionals (the top 20–25% of current AI users) are already doing. The job isn't to become a different type of person. The job is to become the maker for your function — and the community is finally naming that clearly enough to build toward it.
