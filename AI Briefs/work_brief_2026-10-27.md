## 📌 Today's Signal

The common thread today is assessment. Proton is about to test its own staff on how they work with AI, Microsoft is repricing its workforce against AI capex, and Mollick is warning that the tools are hiding the very reasoning a human needs to check. Put together, the advantage is shifting to the person who can show their working: who can explain how they used the tool, where they checked it and why they trusted the output. Shopify's designers who build are the constructive version of the same story, because shipping working product is the most legible evidence of AI fluency there is. Through the architecture-of-work lens, Builder and Editor are merging into one daily habit. The Tuesday action: take one piece of work you did with AI this week and write three lines on what you asked, what you checked and what you changed. That is the answer to the interview, whether or not anyone ever asks.
