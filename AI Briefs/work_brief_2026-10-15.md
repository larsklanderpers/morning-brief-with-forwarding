**"Top Google Exec: AI Moves Fast, Jobs Move Slow. Use the Gap" - Silicon Valley Girl (13 Oct, about 29K views)**
Source: YouTube | [Link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jijc0XokSpQ)

## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

**The task, not the job, becomes the unit of work**
Source: Tech Times (14 Oct 2026) | [Link](https://techtimes.com/articles/329003/20261014/workday-launches-ai-workforce-tool-analyzing-tasks-not-just-job-titles.htm)
At Workday Rising in Las Vegas on 13 Oct, Workday announced Work Intelligence, which breaks each role into component tasks, maps the skills each needs, and tracks which tasks AI is disrupting, augmenting or creating, then models build-versus-hire costs. It is in early access for some HCM customers. Workday's Nikki Helmer: "The basic building block of work is shifting from the job to the task." Workday sells this product, so the framing is a pitch as well as an observation.
💡 Constructive read: This is the architecture-of-work model turning into enterprise software: once HR systems count tasks instead of titles, archetypes stop being a podcast idea and start being how people get staffed. The move for an individual is to do the decomposition before the system does it for you, so you are the one saying which of your tasks are worth keeping.

**The developer who reviews the agent's code**
Source: CNBC (11 Oct 2026) | [Link](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/11/ai-software-developer-jobs.html)
CNBC reports US software development postings on Indeed are up nearly 15% from late February 2025, still 27.5% below pre-pandemic, with senior roles 71% and AI-titled jobs 37% of the increase from May 2025 to May 2026. GitHub says about one in three pull requests now involve an AI agent, against fewer than one in ten a year earlier. The stated reason: companies produce more code than ever and need people to oversee it, evaluate AI output and help clients integrate it. CNBC also cites Stanford data that employment for 22 to 25 year olds in the field fell nearly 20%.
💡 Constructive read: More code means more need for editors, the same way cheap printing made more work for editors, not less. The Editor and Risk Steward archetypes are where the hiring is; the honest catch is that it is senior hiring, so the entry rung still has to be rebuilt by teams that choose to train.

## ⚖️ The Debate (kept honest, kept brief)

**Workday: AI is rewriting jobs more than cutting them**
Source: Workday press release (5 Oct 2026) | [Link](https://investor.workday.com/news-and-events/press-releases/news-details/2026/Workday-Global-Workforce-Report-AI-Is-Rewriting-Jobs-More-Than-Its-Cutting-Them/default.aspx)
Workday's survey of 6,001 employees and leaders found 40% of leaders expect AI to get more from existing staff and 28% expect it to cut headcount. Internal moves fell at 57% of employers, demand for basic AI skills fell 25% from its January peak while hands-on AI skills rose 51% (Sep 2025 to Jul 2026), and median applicants per filled job rose to 69 from 58. Workday's Phil Willburn: "Employees may not be changing jobs, but their jobs are changing around them." Workday sells the HR and skills software this report argues for.
💡 Constructive read: The labour market looks frozen from outside and is churning inside: fewer people moving, more of the job changing under them. The signal for anyone mid-career is in the skills split, where "has used ChatGPT" is losing value and "has built something with it" is gaining.

## 📌 Today's Signal

The job is being taken apart into tasks, and today that stopped being a metaphor. Workday shipped software that counts tasks instead of titles, Google's own data says AI touches about a fifth of the tasks in a typical job, and developer postings are rising for people who review what agents write. Read together, the shape is not fewer jobs so much as jobs re-cut around which tasks a human still owns, with the editing, judging and second-opinion work gaining value. The practical move: list the ten tasks that make up your week, mark the two you would happily hand to an agent and the two only you can do well, and start talking about your work in terms of the second pair. If your employer's HR system is about to do this decomposition anyway, it is better that your version exists first.
