## 🔒 Lane 4 — Privacy, PII & Data Sovereignty

**Australia Privacy Amendment (Personal Data Protection) Bill 2026 — KEY DEVELOPMENT:**
- Consultation closed Sep 18, 2026
- **"Fair and reasonable test"** for data practices — a world first; shifts burden from notice-and-consent to substantive reasonableness
- **Stronger consent requirements:** voluntary, informed, current, specific, unambiguous; pre-ticked boxes explicitly invalid
- **Expanded definition of personal information:** AI-generated inferences about a person are expressly covered
- **72-hour breach notification requirement**
- **Right to erasure** applies to large digital platforms (2.5M+ Australian users)
- *Relevance:* If any of Lars's clients have AU customers and use AI to generate profiles or inferences, this Bill directly constrains what they can do with that data — even if they never collected the inferences themselves. The "fair and reasonable" test is the privacy equivalent of a general duty of care: hard to game with fine print.

**EU AI Act Digital Omnibus (in force Jul 27, 2026):**
