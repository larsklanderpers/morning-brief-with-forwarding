## ⚖️ The Debate (kept honest, kept brief)

**CESifo: no sign yet of AI in recent-graduate unemployment**
Source: CESifo Working Paper 12994 (Fairlie and Wu) | [ifo](https://www.ifo.de/en/cesifo/publications/2026/working-paper/early-impacts-ai-employment-among-recent-college-graduates)
Using Current Population Survey data, the paper finds unemployment among 22 to 25 year old bachelor's graduates held at 7.3% in June to August 2026, within the range of prior summers, with no significant rise relative to older graduates or young non-graduates. It measures unemployment, not whether young workers are being hired into AI-exposed occupations, so it sits alongside the entry-rung studies rather than overturning them.
💡 Constructive read: The front door is not locked, but that does not tell you which rooms are being furnished. The honest summary is still: no aggregate shock, watch the composition.

**@AndrewYNg (Andrew Ng)** - on the AI-fear narrative (21 Sep) · https://x.com/AndrewYNg/status/2102140576498065758
Ng argued that the recent surge in AI-danger rhetoric reflects "a well orchestrated PR campaign" rather than any dangerous turn in the technology.
💡 Constructive read: His view, stated as such, from someone whose AI Fund and DeepLearning.AI do better when adoption does. Still a useful counterweight in a week of kill-switch headlines: read both camps' claims against the labour data, not each other.

## 📌 Today's Signal

The model-launch noise this week hides a quieter shift in what the work looks like. Jev's second week is about people learning to ask a machine a well-formed question, Opus 5.5's effort dial asks every user to price their own tasks, and Mollick's second prompt shows where the human stays in the loop: sending the draft back with a better note. None of that needs a new title. It is the architecture-of-work model showing up inside existing jobs as Editor, Conductor and Risk Steward moments, and the CESifo data says the entry door is, for now, still open. The move for anyone adapting: pick one recurring decision in your own function, write it as a clean question with a closed set of answers, and you have built your first piece of agent infrastructure.
