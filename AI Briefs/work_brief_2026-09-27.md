**"AI Coding Rate Limits are RIDICULOUS Now - Here's How You Keep Scaling Anyway"** - Cole Medin (23 Sep, 19K views) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZq88JAJSag

💡 Constructive read: The cutting room used to need a room. A non-editor shipping a spec ad from a prompt is "become the maker for your function" arriving in marketing, and the scarce skill left is knowing what a good cut looks like, which is the Editor archetype by name.

💡 Constructive read: The ladder becomes a lattice. Worth reading with the interest named: Cognizant sells the modernisation work this shape requires, so treat it as a thesis from a vendor-CEO, and test it against the entry-rung data rather than taking the flattening as fact.

**@emollick (Ethan Mollick)** - on model personality (27 Sep) · https://x.com/emollick/status/2104039050080342456

💡 Constructive read: Nobody picks a colleague off a CV alone. As agents become coworkers, fit and temperament become part of the selection call, and that judgement belongs to the people who work with them daily, not the procurement sheet.

## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

**AT&T says it will keep hiring people who build and govern agents**
Source: WIRED (as reported) | [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/atandt-is-automating-away-its-old-telecom-empire/)
As reported from CTO Jeremy Legg's interview, AT&T expects some middle-management and junior developer roles to disappear while it keeps hiring people who can develop and govern AI agents, supervise automated processes, and maintain fiber networks.
💡 Constructive read: A named employer describing the new mix in plain words, which is rarer than it should be. Architecture-of-work map: Builder, Risk Steward and Maintainer, with the fiber crew a reminder that the physical Maintainer role is not going anywhere.

**a16z's academy admits on proof of work, not grades**
Source: Fortune | [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/22/a16z-horowitz-andreessen-academy-college-alternative-tuition-biyani/)
The Horowitz Andreessen Academy has raised $42M ($35M from a16z) for an unaccredited, grade-free programme; the first 50 students start in San Francisco in fall 2027, tuition-free with $50,000 in compute credits, admitted on what they have built, shipped or earned. Fortune reports later cohorts are meant to pay elite-university prices, and the roughly 50 hiring partners commit to consider students, not to hire a set number.
💡 Constructive read: The portfolio beats the transcript, which is the Prototyper's entry ticket. Name the interest, though: the funders are also the employers and investors who benefit from the pipeline, and "committed to consider" is not a job offer.

## ⚖️ The Debate (kept honest, kept brief)

**AT&T expects a smaller workforce but rejects the 85,000 figure**
Source: WIRED (as reported) | [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/atandt-is-automating-away-its-old-telecom-empire/)
AT&T employed about 130,900 people in June 2026, down from 141,000 at end-2024, and Legg says it will not have the same headcount in five years, driven by AI, self-service, copper retirement by 2029 and a network-operations rebuild. The "85,000 by 2030" circulating is WIRED's extrapolation of the current pace, which AT&T called inaccurate.
💡 Constructive read: Most of this is a copper network being switched off, not a chatbot taking jobs, and headlines that file it all under AI get the cause wrong. The useful read is the mix change inside a shrinking total, not the extrapolated number.

## 📌 Today's Signal

The weekend's community signal is about staffing, not replacement. Medin staffs a coding job across models the way a foreman staffs a site, Herk hands a non-editor a cutting room, Isenberg pitches the tradesperson-owner who runs a small firm on agents, and AT&T, in one of the few plain statements from a large employer, names the hires it still wants: people who build, govern and supervise agents. Each is a person deciding who does which part of the work, human or machine, and what good looks like at the end. That is the Conductor and Editor pair from the architecture-of-work frame, and it is available to anyone this week, not after a reorg. The practical move: take one recurring job you run, split it into plan, do and check, and ask which of the three actually needs your best mind.
