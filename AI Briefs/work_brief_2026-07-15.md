## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

- **Forward-Deployed Engineer** — listings up 800%+; $215K–$310K base; Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, EY hiring. Embeds inside a customer to build agentic workflows for their stack. Maps cleanly to **Builder + Evangelist** — the maker who ships inside someone else's function.
- **AI FinOps Specialist** — born from "The Token Tax": is the AI making money or just consuming tokens? A pure **Risk Steward/Maintainer** hybrid, and proof that agent sprawl creates accounting jobs.
- **Agent Orchestrator** — McKinsey's "agentic organization" framing has generalists orchestrating agents as the default. Consistent with the thread's baseline: **orchestration is a skill everyone acquires, not a department**. (~90K agentic-AI postings in 2026, +280% YoY.)
- **The unnamed $200K internal role** (Nate Herk) — companies building the agent-automation role "from the inside out" before HR names it. The archetype ladder (Prototyper → Builder → Orchestrator) is being climbed faster than titles can be printed.
- **Uber's "agentic pods"** (via AIDB) — not a role but a *unit of org design*: small human-plus-agents teams as the new atomic work structure.

Sources: [Forbes/Box-McKinsey-LinkedIn roles](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sandycarter/2026/06/02/the-20-new-agentic-ai-jobs-box-mckinsey-and-linkedin-all-see-coming/) · [agentic hiring data](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/agentic-ai-hiring-boom-2026) · [FDE hiring](https://www.kore1.com/hire-ai-forward-deployed-engineers-2026/)

The data says transformation, not termination — so far.

## 📌 Today's Signal

The community stopped arguing about whether jobs survive and started publishing operating manuals. Mollick's "twilight of the chatbots" names the shift, Berman and Medin demonstrate it, Herk prices it at $200K, and the hiring data confirms companies are paying for it — while the two loudest apocalypse voices publicly walked their predictions back. The empowering read: the scarce skill this cycle is *management of literal, tireless executors* — defining work, setting quality bars, checking output — and it's acquirable by anyone who already knows their function's workflow cold. The architecture-of-work frame is winning on evidence: roles are decomposing into archetypes faster than titles can keep up, and the people naming their own roles (Herk's unnamed $200K job, Uber's pods) are the ones writing the org chart of 2027. The open problem worth watching — and writing about: rebuilding the apprenticeship ladder so the next generation gets its judgment reps.
