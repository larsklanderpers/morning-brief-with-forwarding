## 🎥 What Creators Are Saying (YouTube)

**"The AI Engineering Skills Map for Knowledge Workers"** — The AI Daily Brief / NLW (18 Aug 2026) · [aidailybrief.ai](https://aidailybrief.ai/)
NLW maps five essential skills for the agentic era: working with agents, building new tools, recognising newly-possible opportunities, and — crucially — grounding all of it in domain judgment AI can't replace. The framing: AI literacy isn't one skill, it's a stack, and the base layer is still expertise.
💡 *Constructive read:* The people who own their function's domain knowledge and layer agent skills on top are not threatened by AI — they become its quality filter. The skill gap is in the stack, not the subject matter.

**"The Job Positions of the AI Future"** — The AI Daily Brief / NLW (5 Jul 2026) · [aidailybrief.ai/e/2026-07-05](https://aidailybrief.ai/e/2026-07-05) · [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5vgmT6wyLVFbsWRdagFdbo)
The anchor episode for this thread. NLW builds on Boris Cherny's Claude Code role archetypes to sketch the full architecture of agentic work: product-facing roles (Prototyper, Builder, Sweeper, Grower, Maintainer) and outward-facing roles (Editor, Scout, Evangelist, Orchestrator, Conductor, Risk Steward). The meta-shift: jobs are dissolving into archetypes that map to temperament more than title. The one move to future-proof yourself: become the maker for your function.
💡 *Constructive read:* If you are currently the person who knows how something is done in your function, you are already halfway to being the Maker. The other half is learning to delegate execution to agents.

**Matthew Berman** — [youtube.com/@matthew_berman](https://www.youtube.com/@matthew_berman/videos)
Berman's own workflow is the demo: agents find video topics, research them, generate thumbnails, extract talking points — all overnight. He wakes to a Slack thread of completed deliverables. No commentary needed; the channel is a live proof of concept for the Orchestrator archetype at consumer scale.
💡 *Constructive read:* When a solo creator runs a five-person workflow with agents, the constraint becomes editorial judgment, not bandwidth. That's a feature, not a threat.

## 🆕 New Roles & Archetypes Watch

**Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** — the breakout role of the year. 42× growth 2023–2025 (vs. 13× for AI Engineer per LinkedIn Jan 2026). Demand up 729% in 12 months. Comp: mid-six figures at mid-level, high six at staff. OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, Mistral, Cohere, Scale all hiring aggressively.
*Architecture-of-work lens:* This is the Builder + Orchestrator hybrid at enterprise scale — someone who embeds in a customer's team, learns their broken workflows, and ships AI solutions on-site. The scarcity is not coding skill; it's the combination of technical and contextual fluency. → [jobsbyculture.com](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/forward-deployed-engineer-boom-2026) · [hashnode.com](https://hashnode.com/blog/a-complete-2026-guide-to-the-forward-deployed-engineer)

**AI Orchestrator** — 739 job listings in February 2026, a 1,294% YoY increase. Role definition: redesigning how work moves across people and AI, deciding when to deploy autonomous agents and when to hand off to humans. The gap an orchestrator fills: agents can write, analyse, and code, but they can't coordinate work between themselves and don't account for what humans do alongside them. That coordination layer is the job. → [eightfold.ai](https://eightfold.ai/blog/most-important-job-2026/) · [itbrew.com](https://www.itbrew.com/stories/ai-orchestrators-are-the-newest-in-demand-ai-role)

**"Agent Boss"** (Microsoft's framing, Work Trend Index 2026) — employees guiding AI outputs, applying judgment, owning results. Microsoft finds only 19% of workers are in the "Frontier zone" (high individual capability + high org readiness). The opportunity: the gap between individual AI capability and organisational redesign is where new roles are forming. → [microsoft.com/worklab](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization)

**Forbes "20 New Agentic AI Jobs"** (Jun 2, 2026) — Box, McKinsey, and LinkedIn converge on a list: AI Strategist, Agent Supervisor, AI FinOps, Algorithm Auditor, Forward-Deployed Engineer, and 15 others. LinkedIn reports 1.3M AI-related job opportunities created in the past two years. AI Engineer is the fastest-growing US job title for the second year running. → [forbes.com](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sandycarter/2026/06/02/the-20-new-agentic-ai-jobs-box-mckinsey-and-linkedin-all-see-coming/)

**EU AI Act Deployer Obligations — live as of 2 August 2026** — employers must now have AI literacy programmes, human oversight, worker notice, and logging in place for high-risk AI systems. "AI literacy" is a legal obligation, not a nice-to-have. Enforcement by national market surveillance authorities begins now. → [dlapiper.com](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/deployer-obligations-under-the-ai-act-implications-for-employers-from-2-august-2026) · [crowell.com](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/part-2-ai-literacy-employer-ai-literacy-obligations-under-the-eu-ai-act)
*Architecture-of-work lens:* The Risk Steward archetype just became a compliance requirement in the EU. Every org with EU exposure needs one.

## ⚖️ The Debate (kept honest, kept brief)

The apocalypse narrative is in retreat. Altman and Amodei both publicly walked back their "50% of white-collar jobs gone" predictions by May 2026 — Altman told a Commonwealth Bank conference he was "pretty wrong" and "delighted to be wrong." a16z called the job-apocalypse framing "unhelpful marketing, bad economics and worse history." Yale Budget Lab's tracker (through March 2026) finds no measurable shift in occupational mix or unemployment duration attributable to AI at the economy-wide level. The ground truth is messier: 115K global tech layoffs by May 2026, entry-level tech hiring down 30–50% at top firms, but mid/senior hiring holding and 1.3M new AI-related roles created. The honest read: disruption is real and uneven, not uniform and apocalyptic. → [fortune.com](https://fortune.com/2026/05/26/sam-altman-dario-amodei-walking-back-ai-jobs-apocalypse-prophecies-ipo/) · [fortune.com/a16z](https://fortune.com/2026/05/07/ai-job-apocalypse-unhelpful-marketing-bad-economics-worse-history/)

## 📌 Today's Signal

The community conversation has quietly crossed a threshold: it's no longer asking whether AI will reshape work, but how fast specific archetypes are being priced into the labour market. The Forward Deployed Engineer's 729% demand surge and the AI Orchestrator's 1,294% job-listing growth are not projections — they are this year's hiring data. NLW's skills-map episode names the underlying logic: domain expertise is the non-negotiable base, and the five agentic skills are the multiplier on top. The workers who understand their function well enough to design the agent stack for it will not compete with AI. They will become the function's architect. The EU AI Act's literacy obligations, now enforceable, are the regulatory signal that this is no longer optional for European employers. The architecture-of-work model isn't just a useful frame — the labour market is now pricing it.
